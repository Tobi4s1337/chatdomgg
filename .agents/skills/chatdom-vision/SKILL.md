# Chatdom vision

Chatdom gives every Twitch channel a persistent kingdom its chat lives in. **The heart is belonging**
("your chat has a home"). Glory, creation and play serve it. The setting is always a fantasy kingdom, flavoured
by its channel. AI is **quiet magic**: Codex founds, forges and voices, and is never the headline.

## Where answers live

1. `chatdom/ChatDom.md`: principles 1–15, people, world, money lines, the never-list, words. Read it first.
2. Each topic file's **"Decided"** list overrides its own body. Read the list before the body.
3. On a conflict, ChatDom.md wins; after that, the newer change-log line wins.
4. `chatdom/Moments.md` holds the signature moments: Genesis, the citizen card, the Forge ritual, the tavern,
   the campfire, the Raid Gate, emotes everywhere, spectacles, seasons, the wardrobe and build mode.
5. Citations of `docs/…` point at research that is not in this repo. The ChatDom file already states the
   decision.

## Founder decisions that are easy to undo (do not drift back)

- Kingdoms are **granted, never paid for**. Nothing that signals loyalty can be bought.
- Bits buy moments and things that last a day. They never leave a permanent thing for the buyer.
- Subs show in three places: sub pieces, an edge tint by tier (gold, rose, rainbow), and the street.
- The tier-3 cape is rainbow and animated: an elegant star shimmer.
- Sub months count toward the street. Subs never raise trust.
- **One person, one vote.** Trust never weights a vote.
- Tenure builds the city. Old viewers live in old quarters near the castle.
- The passport is public, with a hide switch, and built from raids and visits.
- A person lives in at most 3 places. A streamer's own castle doesn't count.
- Every citizen card shows every home. Homes in other kingdoms appear only after the person has signed in once.
- Avatars are always real people. Townsfolk are only a few keepers of places.
- Trade happens only between kingdoms, on visits and raids. At home, things are only given.
- The Forge:
  - It makes house parts, published instantly behind the judges.
  - It runs only while the channel is live.
  - Coals come from showing up: one per night present, with a flat cap of 3.
  - Anvils cap how many forges run at once. A Hype Train roar opens anvils, never coals.
  - A refusal may air as a nameless comic beat, at most once per 10 minutes.
- Genesis (founding a kingdom) happens **live only**, as a 3–4 minute ceremony.
- Only the streamer presses royal acts. The dragon is earned only, never on demand.
- No Twitch player on chatdom.gg. No auto-clips. Chatdom makes no video of its own; Chronicles keep stills only.
- Look and feel:
  - "A handmade toy kingdom at dusk, lit by its people."
  - Crafted-diorama voxels, never Minecraft.
  - Chibi characters (head about 1/3 of the body).
  - A carved-and-crafted UI of wood, brass, parchment and wax seals.
  - Painted interiors.
  - The Atlas is a **day map** of ink and watercolour: a vermilion wash for live channels, a pennant up close,
    small channels as dabs. It has no night and no lights.
- PC controls: a free cursor with the camera following. Nothing needs a held button.
- Twitch sign-in is required to enter a kingdom. The Atlas and the share cards are public.
- Contests do no harm. Nobody can damage, take or lose anything.

## Before you build a feature, check

- Does it serve belonging, and would a four-year lurker feel at home?
- Does it start on Twitch and land in the world (or the reverse)?
- Does it break a money line or a never-list item?
- Is it the toy-kingdom look, or generic game UI?
- Would a streamer show it off on stream? Would chat clip it?
