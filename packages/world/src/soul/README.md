# Kingdom soul

`soulAt(config, epochMs)` is a pure snapshot; for animation loops, create one `createSoulSampler(config)` and call it each frame: it samples motion each time and samples historical weather at the start of each minute, independent of call order. The sampler assumes its config and topology are immutable; construct a new sampler when they change. it owns no clock, timers, persistent state, citizens or Twitch events. Supply the same seed, biome, fixed local UTC offset and epoch to all clients to obtain the same scene, including when nobody sampled it while offline. Hours and meteorological seasons use that offset; the caller owns changes to civil time/DST. Rain, snow, cloud and wind vary slowly from seeded interpolated weather. Wetness and snow cover derive from a five-day fading history; both are normalized presentation weights, not a hydrological forecast. Temperature is in Celsius; all other weather fields are 0–1. Snow cover is reduced as the current temperature warms. `population` is the number of real viewers currently present, not the number of homes. At 12 or more, keepers step inside (`visible: false`); their routines and lamp states continue. Otherwise, the baker delivers every 15 minutes from 06:00 to 11:00, the lamplighter trims lamps every 15 minutes from 15:00 to 16:00 and makes a dusk round at 17:00, and the smith and harbour master are visible on duty from 08:00 to 17:00 and 18:00 respectively. For longer supplied routes the interval grows to allow a complete return. The smith and harbour-master route origins must be authored open-workshop and quay positions. Between trips the baker and lamplighter are indoors. Population changes can immediately hide or reveal a keeper; a presentation may fade that transition. This does not invent citizens or multiply keepers. Seasonal boundaries use March 20, June 21, September 22 and December 21 in local time (calendar approximations of the astronomical events).

`DEFAULT_SOUL_TOPOLOGY` is an authored compact street for an isolated demonstration, not the geometry of a founded kingdom. In a real kingdom, pass `topology` with already navigable connected waypoint polylines for each present keeper, and lamp positions with `routeProgress` measured by distance from zero to one along the lamplighter's outward route. Supply a harbour and route only where the actual kingdom has one. Missing or empty routes omit that keeper. Each route starts at its place, walks out and returns along the same path at 0.55 m/s with three-second waypoint pauses; the caller must ensure every segment clears buildings, slopes and other blockers. Ground and structure placement remain the caller's authority. `heading` is radians around +Y; zero faces +Z. Keepers are labelled by occupation, have no citizen presence light and are never real viewers.

Lamps light as the lamplighter reaches each distance along the outward route after the 17:00 departure. They dim automatically at 05:00 with the dawn: this represents their shared timed dawn mechanism, so a second invented lamplighter trip is not implied. These are the keeper's desired state; compose with the actual kingdom's existing people-driven lighting policy rather than replacing it. `chimneySmoke` and `breadCart` represent the baker's work; attach them to the real bakery and cart, not an arbitrary house. The harbour master's bell is true only for 12 seconds after an explicitly supplied, witnessed arrival epoch. No arrival is inferred from weather or time. An illustrative arrival must be labelled simulated by its presentation.
