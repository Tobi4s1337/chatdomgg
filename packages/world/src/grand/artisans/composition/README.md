# Four places, one clay world

`channelComposition(broadcasterIdOrLogin)` selects an authored composition by full broadcaster ID.
The four profiles deliberately change geometry as well as foliage: tobi4s_ frames a mere and gold
woodland, The_AGGman opens onto a broad harbour, PinkKittyRose follows a cherry-lined river,
and MashaD4 places the castle below a taller pine-and-birch range. These are reviewed art choices
for the four proof channels, not claims about their audiences or invented channel facts.

`createComposition(identity)` is a **SIMULATED unoccupied art study**. It combines the compact kits,
coherent strata, slope-sensitive snow, stepped connected water reaches, fields, groves and coastal
structures into a fixed landscape. All coordinates use the half-metre terrain lattice. Its `hero`,
`street`, `coast`, `dusk` anchors are camera focus points, never movement permissions. `home-N`
anchors cause the review app to show actual accepted cottage assets at their native 0.125 m lattice.
They imply no citizen, ownership, presence or settled lot. Unsupported home envelopes are omitted.

The grand builder consumes the profile as composition constraints on its existing canonical
field; it must retain its Genesis seed, compiler identity, approved layout and exact installed
placements. Do not install this review study over an existing kingdom. Do not replace authored
homes/castle/Forge with its boxes. Independent feature streams keep grove detail from moving a
river or castle. Materials are broad coherent masses; voxel-sized speckle and glow were rejected.

The isolated bench has static water and a deliberately small landscape; its pictures cannot
prove production LOD, hydrology, collision, shadows or real-GPU timing. That evidence belongs to
the kit's adoption into `/-/grand` and the builder's streaming renderer.
