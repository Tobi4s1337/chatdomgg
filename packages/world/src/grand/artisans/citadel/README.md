# Citadel and town-wall studies

`createCitadel(seedKey)` returns a seeded, unoccupied silhouette on a tiered rock in
half-metre integer cells; its axis is +Y and its entrance faces −Z. Its native-rock
base uses offset strata and broad moss ledges under a single worked stone plinth. The clustered
indigo roofs and warm stone courses are restrained rather than copying the reference's
blue-and-white castle. Banners are solid-colour cloth reserved for a validated channel
crest. `base`, `gate` and `summit` are visual framing hints.

`createTownWalls(seedKey)` returns a matching 148-cell-square wall with a visibly open
16-cell gate facing −Z. `gate` and `gateInterior` locate the opening for composition.
The opening is geometrically empty, but these anchors do not assert navigability.

The kits contain coloured boxes for review/meshing, not public scene content or
colliders. They do not replace the installed castle, citizen homes, terrain support,
Forge, or their identities and placements. The builder must fit any adopted dressing
around the actual castle/settlement and preserve existing entrances and support. This
study's portico is a short exterior recess only, not a passable castle doorway. No
window is emissive and no simulated person or presence is implied.
