# Geological and water studies

`createGeologySampler(seedKey)` caches seed hashing, and `.column(x, z)` caches the band warp for all cells in that column. `sampleGeology` is its convenient scalar equivalent. They give the existing grand voxeliser's stone IDs (`14..29`, `30`, `32`, `33`) from world coordinates, terrain slope, exposure and seed. It keeps continuous slow strata through chunk edges, snow on ledges near/above the snowline, bare steep rock, and a caller-selected talus apron. The caller supplies the actual top solid voxel (`surfaceY`) and the actual terrain slope; it must not use a far LOD as a physical sampler. The accompanying colour table is for the isolated granite review; production should use its kingdom-specific palette.

`createGeologyReview(seedKey)` produces an isolated seeded study of two warped, connected spines, coherent rock folds, high crags, snow and scree. `createRangeHeightField(seedKey)` exposes its world-aligned height samples for review only; it is not the grand generator or a physical terrain source. `createWatercourse(seedKey)` produces a material study with a highland mere feeding a descending connected channel, widening mouth, waterfall and pool, beach and layered sea stacks. `watercourseProfile()` exposes the study's cross-sections. All coordinates are 0.5 m terrain cells. The water boxes are static presentation geometry, **not** a drainage solver, collision, a water physics model or proof of a real installed land; use the grand builder's canonical drainage/water fields and animated water owner for production. No warm windows or invented presence are introduced.

Review geometry is not designed to be stamped over founded support, roads or lots. Bind material sampling and water geometry only where the builder's authoritative terrain and hydrology allow it; do not duplicate the review island in the world.
