# Forge motifs

These are build-lattice sculptures, not living creatures: the dragon is a low, curled chimney carving with closed eyes; the crescent cradles a glass lantern; the owl is a shallow cast vane perched on an arrow. Each is mounted by its bottom contact. The returned emitter is a placement anchor, not an emissive voxel: outside the dragon's muzzle, in the lantern glass, and just in front of the owl.

Hearth uses warm terracotta, brick and limestone with a rounded crown; Tide uses slate, cream and a swept crest; Grove uses moss, bark and thatch with a leafy crest. Their wing and crown profiles differ as well as their registered materials. Lantern tips and owl shoulders/ears similarly take the kit's shape language. No palette entries are introduced.
