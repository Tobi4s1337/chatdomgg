# Reference: multiplayer character presentation

For: Chatdom's walking client, citizen pose and camera. Source reading only; no demo, captures, measured latency or performance claims. The companion notes cover the SDK's interpolation details and voxel support separately.

## Sources

- [Colyseus Defold platformer](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/tree/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74), MIT, studied at `64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74`. An instructive 3D example of a fixed-step body, a separate interpolated visual, and remote Predict values; its movement authority is deliberately unlike ours.
- [NJ MMO](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/tree/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9), MIT, studied at `7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9`. A Three.js MMO with state-driven avatar clips and click-to-move; useful contrast because its player pose is directly assigned on state changes.

## What we take

- **Keep simulation and visible presentation distinct, with the camera following the same visible local pose.** Defold's body runs at 60 Hz while a separate object-interpolation target carries the model between fixed steps; the camera follows that visual in late update ([manual](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/PLATFORMER.md#L33-L65), [player](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/prefabs/player/player.script#L428-L448)). Respawn resets both physical position and interpolation history, avoiding a visible trip across the map. For our server placement or collision revision change, reset presentation history deliberately too; do not interpolate a teleport. Camera obstruction remains mandatory in Chatdom.
- **Remote past is intentional latency, not correction of the local player.** Defold attaches Predict only to remote players; x/y/z use linear interpolation with 120 ms delay and 4-unit snap; yaw uses angular interpolation. It ticks Predict each frame, and the remote reads `predict:value` ([setup](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/game.script#L11-L18), [ownership and detach](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/game.script#L64-L90), [read](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/prefabs/player/player.script#L313-L325)). Its comment relates 120 ms to 50 ms patches and jitter; it is an example, not a universal target. The local model is locally simulated and streams its transform; this sample does **not** demonstrate authoritative local reconciliation.
- **Make camera smoothing time-based and track winding angles.** Defold uses `1 - exp(-rate * dt)` at position rate 10/s, rotation 20/s and zoom 10/s, stores current yaw separately to avoid the ±180° quaternion-to-Euler wrap, and updates after the visual ([camera](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/camera_follow.script#L3-L15), [smoothing](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/camera_follow.script#L99-L144)). Additional follow lag can separate camera and body during corrections, so validate it in motion before borrowing rates.
- **Use actual travel to drive gait, and blend state changes.** Defold selects idle/walk/jump/fall and blends changes over 0.1 s, with replicated animation state for remote effects ([constants](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/prefabs/player/player.script#L42-L55), [transition](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/prefabs/player/player.script#L100-L111), [remote effects](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/prefabs/player/player.script#L327-L347)). Its walking dust runs every 0.16 s; this is timer-based, not proof of planted steps. NJ identifies movement from >0.02 units per sync and coasts 200 ms before idle, with a 0.18 s clip crossfade ([thresholds](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/scene/player-avatar.ts#L19-L21), [remote](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/scene/remote-player-avatar.ts#L92-L130), [crossfade](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/scene/creature/mesh-character.ts#L20-L21)). Threshold-per-patch and grace timers vary with patch cadence and can keep walking after stopping. They are useful anti-flicker examples, not a foot-contact solution. NJ footsteps require both 0.8 m and 350 ms ([trigger](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/audio/audio-triggers.ts#L9-L19)); align future Chatdom sounds with planted feet and surface, rather than copying those thresholds.
- **Pointer lock is a gesture with an exit.** Defold captures on an active touch/click and Escape releases ([input](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/game/camera_follow.script#L153-L175)). This native window sample does not show browser rejection, lock error recovery or a quiet free-cursor fallback. Retain Chatdom's explicit opt-in and working drag orbit when lock is unavailable.

## Chatdom comparison and decisions

- `apps/web/src/realm/client.ts` already uses pinned Colyseus Predict: remote `lerp` with 250 ms delay and 4-unit snap, local reconciler and shared movement. Preserve this single owner of input history and reconciliation; never add a second queue or copy Defold's client-submitted transforms. Its remote values feed `apps/web/src/walk/index.ts` and `apps/web/src/kingdom/walk-scene.ts` once per frame. Test packet jitter and changed placement/collision revisions against camera and feet together; neither reference establishes that our 250 ms is optimal.
- `apps/web/src/walk/index.ts` takes the self render pose as camera anchor and adjusts body and camera height for steps; it resets step easing on collision revision or placement changes. Inspect whether Predict's own visual history and the distance/gait phase also reset at teleports, instead of assuming a camera reset handles them.
- `apps/web/src/citizens/index.ts` already derives gait phase from rendered distance, 0.75 m per cycle, with a solved leg-height adjustment and opposing limbs. That is a better starting point for ground contact than either timer or patch-delta gate; inspect walk, stop, corrections and steps in motion for sliding and transition pops. The studies provide no tested bob amplitude or demonstrated no-slide result.
- `apps/web/src/walk/input/look.ts` already opts in with V, releases with Escape, retries without raw movement and falls back to drag orbit on refusal; pointer warps are filtered. Preserve Escape, warp filtering and drag orbit; the founder’s newer review requires lock acquisition only from a real click, without repeated retries or browser-error spam. The existing V acquisition/retry path is evidence to inspect, not behavior to preserve. No browser-fallback technique was found in the examples.

## Leave behind and limits

Defold's [server copies the latest sanitized client transform](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/server/src/rooms/PlatformerRoom.ts#L28-L35) ([step](https://github.com/colyseus/defold-platformer-game/blob/64f67b150ab65fb26b6dfc475aa7b75462e1ce74/server/src/rooms/PlatformerRoom.ts#L73-L85)); that is not server-authoritative physics or a correction algorithm. NJ routes state changes into `sync`, which directly assigns player position and yaw; its camera directly follows local state ([network](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/net/room.ts#L1277-L1315), [remote pose](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/scene/remote-player-avatar.ts#L92-L130), [camera](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/client/src/scene/renderer.ts#L283-L294)). No snapshot interpolation or client reconciliation was found in those player paths. Its [click-to-move step](https://github.com/tech-leads-club/nj-mmo/blob/7bba7272386357b8d5c19dd82906d13bd49f1bb9/libs/game-core/src/movement-system.ts#L14-L55) uses speed 8 and arrival epsilon 0.05; it is not Chatdom's collision controller. Do not substitute its placeholder avatar, art, or movement rules. Source reading does not establish visual quality, input latency, browser behavior, or real-device frame rate.
