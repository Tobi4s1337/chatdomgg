# Reference: Colyseus predicted render smoothing

For the walk controller and kingdom camera. Studied [prediction playground](https://github.com/colyseus/prediction-playground/tree/90d7dd247158d18a1d9b024ecc1087a7de5ddf2e) at `90d7dd2` and [Colyseus SDK](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/tree/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk) at `724b544`. Both are MIT. The installed `apps/web/node_modules/@colyseus/sdk` reports 0.18.8; its `src/predict/{Predictor,rollback,reconciler}.ts` matches the studied Colyseus checkout byte for byte. The separate 0.18.8 package reference contains `Predictor.ts` and `rollback.ts` but not `reconciler.ts`; the installed package supplies the latter. These findings are for this installed version, not a promise about every SDK release.

## What it does

[Lab 03](https://github.com/colyseus/prediction-playground/blob/90d7dd247158d18a1d9b024ecc1087a7de5ddf2e/src/client/labs/03-reconcile/net.ts#L7-L45) shares its server movement function, tracks position **and velocity**, predicts each reliable input synchronously, and on acknowledgement adopts the server state and replays pending inputs. Its [frame loop](https://github.com/colyseus/prediction-playground/blob/90d7dd247158d18a1d9b024ecc1087a7de5ddf2e/src/client/labs/03-reconcile/index.ts#L88-L102) ticks once, sends every due fixed input, then draws `recon.value()`; its toggle draws `recon.state` to reveal the difference ([lines 143–148](https://github.com/colyseus/prediction-playground/blob/90d7dd247158d18a1d9b024ecc1087a7de5ddf2e/src/client/labs/03-reconcile/index.ts#L143-L148)). It uses 20 Hz simulation, 65 ms correction decay, and a teleport threshold of 8 arena units; these are demo parameters, not metre-scale tuning recommendations ([constants](https://github.com/colyseus/prediction-playground/blob/90d7dd247158d18a1d9b024ecc1087a7de5ddf2e/src/shared/constants.ts#L1-L16), [controls](https://github.com/colyseus/prediction-playground/blob/90d7dd247158d18a1d9b024ecc1087a7de5ddf2e/src/client/labs/03-reconcile/index.ts#L32-L79)).

The installed SDK's reconciler keeps exact local state separate from presentation. Each fixed send saves `prev = exact + error`, steps the state, and consumes a fixed interval from its render accumulator. On every frame `alpha = clamp(renderAcc / stepMs, 0, 1)` and `value = prev + alpha * (exact + error - prev)` ([rollback](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L618-L655), [value](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/reconciler.ts#L222-L235)). At 30 Hz, a fixed step is 33.33 ms; at 360 Hz there are about twelve rendered samples per step. The interpolation adds roughly one fixed step of visual latency, but avoids a visible 30 Hz staircase without slowing physics or chasing every position with an extra spring. When no steps arrive alpha clamps at 1 and holds; the SDK resynchronizes the accumulator on subsequent sends.

On a mismatch, the SDK saves the prior non-interpolated `exact + error`, adopts truth, replays unacknowledged inputs, then sets `error = saved - new exact`, keeping the prior fixed-step interpolation endpoint intact. Every render tick multiplies error by `exp(-dt / smoothMs)` ([reconcile](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L711-L750), [decay](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L547-L592)). An 80 ms time constant removes about 63% in 80 ms and 95% in 240 ms; it is not an 80 ms linear blend. Wire-precision-matching acknowledged states skip adopt/replay to avoid correction noise at collision branch boundaries ([source](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L676-L708)).

## What we take

- One frame driver: pass the actual `requestAnimationFrame` timestamp, call `predict.tick(now)`, send exactly its due input count, then read every render pose; only afterward place camera and draw the world. Sampling `performance.now()` inside rAF adds scheduling jitter; reading before sending can flat-top a frame. The 0.18.8 driver caps catch-up at five steps and drops excess after a hitch ([source](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/Predictor.ts#L2200-L2250)).
- Use the *same rendered local pose* for body and camera translation, and use exact `reconciler.state` for collision, grounded decisions, gameplay and replay. `predict.value(self, field)` is explicitly overlaid by the live reconciler; it resolves to its interpolated, correction-smoothed `value`, not the passive remote snapshot track. On dispose it restores the prior track or raw fallback ([binding](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/Predictor.ts#L2482-L2500), [read](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/Predictor.ts#L2568-L2588)). Do not feed render values back into `stepPlayer` or the boom's simulation collision state.
- Corrections should be bounded visual offsets, not an always-on EMA chase that permanently trails movement. Snap known placements/reset replay anchors on the placement serial or collision revision; still sweep the camera against the current collision world. A distance threshold alone cannot identify a short relocation through a wall.
- Track yaw as a wrapped angle or use the exact byte deliberately; do not linearly interpolate byte 255 to 0 through 127. Preserve all state fields required for deterministic replay, including velocity and grounded state.

## Current seams to investigate

1. [client.ts](../../apps/web/src/realm/client.ts) already ticks, sends, and reads in the correct order, and [walk/index.ts](../../apps/web/src/walk/index.ts) already passes rendered `self.x/y/z` into the rig. The kingdom forwards the rAF timestamp, and renders the returned body pose after camera placement. Therefore an assertion that its whole camera follows raw prediction would be false. The 250 ms lerp setting is for passive remote tracks; the live self overlay has its own one-step interpolation and 80 ms correction decay.
2. `snap: 2` on the reconciler is **not positional distance** in SDK 0.18.8: its max absolute correction covers *every numeric declared field* and pops all smoothed fields, including `prev` ([options](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L307-L320), [implementation](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L740-L766), [numeric fields](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/reconciler.ts#L175-L183)). Our declared fields include byte yaw, velocity, accumulated distance and revision as well as metres. A large discrepancy in one of those can pop the entire pose, even when position correction is small. This is a plausible seam, not proof it occurs: instrument the per-field correction and actual discontinuities. Preserve fields needed for replay; do not simply omit them to change snap behavior. The placement serial provides an explicit discontinuity signal, so consider eliminating the all-field threshold or using a position-only mechanism that does not add a second input protocol.
3. [step-ease.ts](../../apps/web/src/walk/motion/step-ease.ts) receives exact predicted y once per frame and classifies grounded-to-grounded height changes as steps, with 70 ms body and 100 ms camera time constants. [walk/index.ts](../../apps/web/src/walk/index.ts) adds its offsets to rendered y. Fixed-step exact height and grounded changes can therefore inject 30 Hz changes into a camera otherwise following interpolated y, especially over fine slopes or landing transitions; inspect separately on flat ground, stairs and jump. Do not smooth a jump twice or allow visual body/near-plane clipping. Body and camera step offsets intentionally differ, so check screen-space relative movement, not only their world positions.
4. `smoothMs: 80` can hide genuine mismatches but repeated mismatch produces visible drag. Measure correction sizes, cadence, predicted versus authoritative history and the time series before increasing or adding camera damping. SDK diagnostics `lastCorrection`/magnitude are conditional on diagnostics or divergence settings in this version; do not assume they update in production merely because `snap` is enabled ([source](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L694-L706), [source](https://github.com/colyseus/colyseus/blob/724b5449b51dcc07b7835afd7b3e4898fda2c7f7/packages/sdk/src/predict/rollback.ts#L745-L776)). Check frame pacing, collision and boom recovery separately: this note does not establish the cause of the reported sponginess.

## Visible acceptance

On a real 360 Hz display, walk continuously past a stationary high-contrast post on level ground in both shoulder and first-person views, start, stop, reverse, then climb a kerb and jump. The post must move smoothly with no visible 30 Hz stepping or patch-rate pulses; the local body must stay steadily framed, and start/stop must feel direct. Record frame timestamp, exact and rendered pose, camera position, placement/revision and correction events alongside the run; repeat at 60 Hz and under an induced correction. A real placement must cut immediately without crossing a wall. A headless screenshot alone cannot demonstrate this temporal behavior.
