## The part
Raids are the moment kingdoms meet, and on tobi4s_ both directions work for real.

**A raid comes in.** Within seconds of Twitch's `channel.raid`, the kingdom's gate wakes. Braziers catch, the raider's standard is hoisted, and a caravan rolls through under the raider's real banner and colours, sized by the log of Twitch's viewer count. The stream camera frames it. At 5 viewers, a raid of 3 is the night's biggest moment: one treasured wagon, lingering close. At 50,000, a raid of 10,000 is a column that takes about a minute to pass the gate, drawn with bounded meshes and aggregate movement, never thousands of invented people. The crier posts one welcome line in tobi4s_'s chat with the kingdom link. Raiders who open it sign in with one click and walk in at the gate as themselves, in their own look, wearing the raiding kingdom's colours and crest. They are greeted by name and can walk, wave and visit, but a visit never creates a home or any standing. Raiders who only chat stay lights (decision 13): when the kingdom can tie a chatter to the raid, a lantern lights on the caravan and their bubble carries the raider's crest. The raider's dated pennant joins the banner wall by the gate, and a road is published for the Atlas. Everyone the kingdom saw arrive with the raid earns a passport stamp from witnessed evidence. There is no tribute (decision 45).

**The kingdom rides out.** At the end of a stream the streamer presses Raid Gate in the dock. The Atlas opens in gate mode: friends with roads first, then smaller live channels, never paid ordering. The preview shows the destination and the real number of walkers who chose to march. One press starts Twitch's countdown. In the 3D world (not on paper), the real walkers in their own looks gather at the kingdom's actual gate, lit by its torches, with banners, and the streamer leads. On Twitch's confirmation the kingdom recedes into ink, the camera rises to the Atlas and the torch column rides the road. Marchers arrive at the destination's gate if it is founded, or at its waiting land with "Watch on Twitch" and a tent pledge. Nothing is posted in the destination's chat (decision 46). Anyone can come home whenever they like.

**What already exists.** Much of the server side is built and tested but never bound to the real system: preview, consent, departure only on a witnessed raid, the durable Twitch outbox, fenced transfer and return, and visitor gifts on Postgres (RG01, RG07–RG10). `createRaidGateService` is not mounted in `apps/api/src/main.ts`, its tables are not a registered migration, and no real room or item transport is bound. Herald subscribes `channel.raid` only with `to_broadcaster_user_id` (`apps/herald/src/ingest/subscriptions.ts:68`), so outgoing raids are never heard. `channel:manage:raids` is not in Herald's `BROADCASTER_SCOPES` (`apps/herald/src/auth/grants.ts`), so assume tobi4s_ has not granted it. Passport travel is hard-coded empty (`apps/api/src/public/panel/reading.ts:188`). The incoming raid today is one wagon between two braziers on a bare street (`work/evidence/live-twitch/raid@1920x1080.png`). The departure is flat paper figures over the 3D town (`work/evidence/raid-road/before-departure-1920.png`, `after-gate-1920.png`). Those three stills are the "before".

You own this end to end and make the design calls. The bar is a raid you would clip, at stream distance, on a real GPU.

## Decisions
- **2026-11-02 — RS12: stamps come from the gate's own history, in the Atlas's arms; the choice lives beside them.** The passport reads `realm_raid_guest_stamps` directly (one REPEATABLE READ snapshot with the holder's choice), so no second ledger exists. A kingdom is named as the newest witnessed raid named its channel and drawn in the same channel-ID arms the Atlas and the caravan use, folded into the passport's crest grammar (heater, the Atlas's two pigments, the Atlas charge or its partition line mapped to oak, star, wave or mountain); a founded kingdom's Genesis crest is not used, so a channel keeps one face across the map, the caravan and the book. The stamp is pressed in the arms of the kingdom it was pressed in (the frozen schema names the destination); the raider's home shows as "By the road from …" where the card already names it. "Hide my travels" is a new `0011f` table rather than a card-side flag, because the card source has no store of its own and the gate must close its own pages even before a host folds the choice in. Gifts, the shutter, Shield Mode and raiders' makings stay parked (Tobias, 17:10).
- **2026-11-02 — RS11: the walker's road rides on the Atlas when the Atlas is drawn.** The walk warms atlas-map's gate mode as soon as the walker may travel (joined, may follow, under way or abroad), unseen behind the journey layer, so the rise and road are drawn on the real map from their first step. A journey uses the map only if the map said it was drawn before its road began; otherwise it keeps the parchment road for the whole journey, never switching halfway, never waiting on a map to step through. The benches take `&map=atlas` (gate mode for the monarch with `finder=1`, the walker's road) over the rehearsed road's real channels, for integrated captures.
- **2026-11-02 — RS11: the walker rides out from the walking view and walks in at the destination's gate.** The march call is play's HUD piece (top-centre) with a walking key (G) for its one forward action, so a walker with the mouse captured can answer. While the authority holds a place, the world washes into ink and parchment and a parchment road draws the rise and the road inside it when the Atlas is absent (the Atlas travel stages still need binding); the page walks on through the ordinary door only once the destination acknowledged the step, never before the rise and road were seen. Abroad, the home gate answers (session memo as a hint, `whereabouts` as the truth), so the way home is offered where the walker stands. On a phone the road bench's walker is called by the march call, as in the product; the throne's board stays the monarch's.
- **2026-11-02 — RS11: Herald holds the raid control; the API holds none.** Start a Raid needs the broadcaster's user token, which Herald used to discard. `herald authorize --role broadcaster` now keeps the refresh token sealed (AES-256-GCM under `HERALD_TOKEN_KEY`, outside the repo) when the raid scope is granted, and Herald's private `/internal/herald/raid-gate` route is the only Twitch writer: one durable claim per gate outbox key before the call, never a second write, reconcile from its ledger. The API's gate binds it as transport, grant, consent and live ports (`bind.ts`) and rereads gate tables for open streams so the realm's landing reaches them. Consent means a stored, unrevoked grant. Destinations stay `unknown` until the Atlas offers a server classification read; Twitch refuses what it would not allow. The dock's press lives in the throne (monarch only) and mounts the existing gate board; the map binds through `import.meta.glob` so this checkout builds without atlas-map's embed and the merged build gets gate mode.
- **2026-11-02 — RS11: travel lives in the realm's rooms, and the party is a public frame.** The API's coordinator reaches the realm only through a private door (`/internal/realm/raid-travel`, `REALM_INTERNAL_SECRET`), and the realm answers from its actual rooms over a durable ledger (`RealmRoomTravel`: ticket per journey, membership with a fencing generation, capacity under one advisory lock per destination). Readiness retains the destination's writer and finds a gate place; a commit fences the source seat through the rooms' own once-a-second session check and is acknowledged only after the room has ended that seat, so no arrival is ever reported while the walker still stands at home. Without the door, travel is unbound and answers unavailable. Marchers to a founded kingdom enter through the RS10 gate admission rather than a second path, dressed under the source banner from the witnessed raid. The gathering is drawn from `GET /realm/raid-party/:kingdomId`, a public frame built only from durable gate records (monarch first, 40 drawn, the rest a count): the stream view and walkers see the same party, nothing comes from chat or a browser claim, and a viewer count never invents a marcher. The party layer draws its own bodies at the gate and names the walker IDs it draws so play can hide their ordinary bodies.
- **2026-11-02 — The column comes down the road.** Where the land in front of the gate is open (lots, squares, the castle and water cleared by a wagon's half-width), units come down up to 48 m of road that bends gently away from the standard, rising out of the dusk at its far end; gate times and the camera are unchanged. A long column now reads by its length from the town-side crane, and a single wagon comes alone. Without such ground they gather at the gate as before. Details in SEAMS §11.
- **2026-11-02 — The walking raider's own view is staged on a private bench, through the production entry.** `/-/raid-gate?guestWalk=<welcome path>` mounts the same welcome page as `/raid/...`, with `origin: 'simulated'`. The adapter compares the kingdom's entry info, the committed guest and the room against that one origin, so the live route still admits only a live kingdom and a bench never mixes the two. The kingdom entry's rehearsal description stays in the data but out of the picture (decision 25). The RS10 stage drives it with a third raider signed in on the SIMULATED provider (`RAID_SHOW_VIEW=walker`). The RS13 run remains the proof on a live origin.
- **2026-11-02 — The gate shot keeps its foreground open.** On the real GPU, the envelope-fitted crane view put a home's roof across a third of the picture. The plan check now also judges the picture when the shot asks: rays through a 9×6 grid, marched to a depth along the view of 80% of the way to the aim (at most 40 m), with homes counted to a 10 m roof. More than 8% blocked refuses the view. Fitted views are tried at that limit, then at 20% and 35%, then on standing alone, then the loose views, so the least obstructed fitted view wins and a dense street still gets a gate shot. A street lined with houses on both sides keeps a roof at the side of the picture; only the scene's own sightline can do better.
- **2026-11-02 — Banner ponies carry the column's length.** Caparisoned, riderless ponies are led two abreast between wagons (no rows for one wagon, one row up to five wagons, two from six), each in the raider's saddle cloth with a pennant of its arms. They are kept only where they fit on the actual road at the column's pace, with at least 0.3 m of air either way, and share one pony budget (120). A first-time viewer sees one continuous procession rather than wagons with gaps; nobody rides and no person is invented. Torch cages are blackened timber and copper, so they read warm. Captions take their own width (capped by the picture), so a phone never breaks a name mid-word.
- **2026-11-02 — The arrival is seen from the town, and scale lives in the world.** The gate shot stands beside the actual street inside the gate and looks back out through the standard, so the column comes towards the viewer; outside-in views are fallbacks only. The town lights a torch avenue (1/2/5/6 pairs at 3/140/10,000/70,000) on conservatively supported ground, and columns of 10,000+ release bounded sky lanterns. No viewer count is aired, and nothing invents a person. Native torch light/occupancy and the gate-party/HUD mounts are exact needs in SEAMS §11.
- **2026-11-02 — All dated pennants can be read.** The existing observation ledger now pages incoming history under a stable commit ceiling, without a second archive store. A bounded parchment reader selects twelve historical bays at a time and restores the latest live wall on close; it never stages an arrival. Numeric ordering is qualified to avoid sorting the textual sequence alias, with decimal-boundary/restart regressions. ## How to work
- **Protocol.** Follow `$ralph-lane`, with goals in place of tasks: one goal at a time, in order. A goal can take many sessions. End each session with everything building, one progress line `<id>: <what + decisions + doubts> — <sha> — <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM>` and a `next: RSxx <done / remaining>` line, and a commit on `lane/raid-show` (`feat(raid-show): RSxx …`). Tick a goal only when its done-when bar is truly met. Merge `main` when it has new commits (`git merge --no-edit main`) and resolve conflicts only inside scope. Never push and never touch `main`.
- **Monday first.** Reach a reviewable RS10 checkpoint early tonight (subscriptions, durable ingest, a first look of the caravan), then keep improving it. The integrator merges reviewed checkpoints continuously from the start.
- **Mock EventSub.** Twitch CLI 1.1.24 is at `/root/.local/bin/twitch`. Sign webhook deliveries to Herald's mock door (`apps/herald/src/mock/door.ts`; `scripts/live/raid.mjs` is a worked example), for example `twitch event trigger channel.raid -F http://127.0.0.1:<door>/… -s <secret> -t 129634568 -f <raider id>`. Use `-f 129634568` for an outgoing raid. Use the CLI's mock API or a recording transport for Start/Cancel Raid. No mock run ever touches real Twitch.
- **Twitch writes only to tobi4s_.** Welcome lines go to tobi4s_'s chat, and Start/Cancel only go out from tobi4s_. Real writes happen only for a real raid in RS13, with Tobias present. Nothing is ever posted in another channel's chat.
- **Ports and services.** Run your servers on free ports in 5170–5186, checking each one first: the mock door, your Herald, your realm and your API. During a GPU lease, serve the web build on 5192 and your realm or API on 5191, or proxy them through the 5192 preview. Never use 5190, 5193–5197 or 5480. Do not restart or reconfigure the live Herald (5194) or realm (5197), and do not create a competing EventSub consumer on the real conduit.
- **Postgres.** Use your own schema (for example `raid_show_test`) on `DATABASE_URL`, loaded with `--env-file`, and never the live schema. Gated suites run with `REALM_DATABASE_TESTS=1`.
- **Secrets.** Never print or read the values in `~/.config/chatdom/*.env`, the repo's `.env` or your own home config. Load them with `--env-file` or `source` without echoing.
- **Real GPU.** Tobias's Chrome on his RTX 5080 is reachable through `pc-lease` (`chatdom-pc-lease`; read its header). It serves one lane at a time for at most 10 minutes. Start your servers inside the leased command, then call `chatdom-pc shot http://localhost:5192/<path> <out.png> --size 1920x1080` (always the full URL; pages signal `window.__chatdomReady`). Take a few stills per lease, or one short clip. If the tunnel is down (exit 3), take one short SwiftShader still under the shared capture lock, held under 5 minutes. SwiftShader frame times are never performance evidence. Look at every still with `$look` before you decide it is good. Keep PNGs under about 2 MB and clips under about 10 MB, in `work/evidence/raid-show/`.
- **Server load.** The host is shared: 12 cores, with Tobias's other services. Typecheck and test only the packages and files you touched. Never run the full suite or `pnpm verify`. Run builds niced, and make no long recordings on the CPU.
- **No SIMULATED labels** on any surface. Public surfaces show real data or nothing.
- **Seams.** Read `SEAMS.md`. Cross a seam only through it: write a `needs:` line with the exact patch and keep going on the parts that do not depend on it. Where a seam is not there yet, show the honest unavailable state, never a stand-in that pretends.
- **Repo text.** No thread IDs, and no tool, model or orchestration names, in commits or files.
- **Same failure three times:** write `blocked:` with what you learned and move to the next independent part.
