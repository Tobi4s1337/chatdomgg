## The part
The grand kingdom is a whole voxel landscape you would screenshot; the builder makes it stream and render at scale, you make every part of it beautiful. When you are done, a still from any kingdom stands next to the Eldermere reference (`/tmp/chatdom-refs/grand/grand-ref-1..6.jpg`) without embarrassment, and it is ours: our clay, our palette, our homes, our dusk.
- **Forests you can tell apart from across the valley:** species with their own silhouettes (autumn gold broadleaf, dark pine, cherry, a fantasy teal, birch, willow by the water), grown by rules into thousands of trees with variation, clustered as real forests with edges, clearings and undergrowth.
- **Mountains with a geology:** rock strata banding the cliffs, scree, snow that settles by slope and height, jagged peaks that read as a range.
- **Water that tells you where it goes:** rivers that widen to their mouths, waterfalls off the plateaus, a mere, beaches, sea stacks and surf lines.
- **The made land:** patchwork fields (wheat, lavender, cabbages) with hedges and windmills, the harbour with quays, ships and a lighthouse, the walled town and the castle on its rock at the scale where spires and banners read from far away.
- **Four kingdoms, four places:** tobi4s_, The_AGGman (a harbour city), PinkKittyRose (a river town), MashaD4 (a mountain city): each composed as its own landscape from its channel, so no two look alike.
Each kit comes with fixed hero, street, coast and dusk views you have looked at, then a moving orbit across the LOD distances. Research on world generation at this scale: `/tmp/chatdom-refs/grand/worldgen-at-scale.md`. Fan the volume out to sub-agents on terra high, one kit folder each, and judge every result by looking. You own it end to end and make the design calls.
Captures: take the shared capture lock for one still or one short clip at a time and release it in between; while you wait for it, keep building, and never end a turn blocked on it.
