# The anvil and the sleeping dragon: art brief

Units: 1 cell = 0.125 m, h = half a cell (used for edges and cuts). The horn points +X and the mark faces +Z. The
work surface is at 1.43 m, nothing goes above 1.50 m and the radius stays within 1.5 m.

## Anvil

**Proportions** (18 cells long):
- **Block:** stone, 12 × 9 × 4 cells, sunk 1 h into the cobbles. The top tilts 1 h, the edges are chamfered 1 h, one
  corner is chipped, and there is a stain ring under the feet.
- **Feet:** 11 × 6 cells and 1.5 tall, with arches of air on the sides (4 × 1) and ends (2 × 1).
- **Waist:** 6 × 3 cells at its narrowest, hourglass in profile. Its flat +Z cheek carries the mark.
- **Face plate:** 2 cells thick and 4 wide, overhanging the waist by 1 h. The heel and face run 11 cells, then a
  2-cell step drops 1 h.
- **Horn:** 5 cells, 3 × 3 at the root and 1 h at the tip. The top is flat and the belly is one rising curve. Never a
  cone.
- **Hardy hole:** 1 × 1 cell, 1 cell from the heel, open right through with dark inner walls. A 1 h pritchel hole
  sits 1.5 cells inboard.

**Silhouettes:**
- **Side:** a flat top, the step, the horn drooping to its point, the hourglass waist, arched feet and a wider
  block.
- **End:** a T (wide face, thin waist, flared feet) with the horn tip as a dot.

**Materials:**
- Near-black, cool blue-grey iron, matched to the engraving's metal so the plaque vanishes.
- Bright steel wear on three places only: the front face edge nearest the horn, the bruised heel corners and the
  horn tip.
- Rust only in the waist hollow and on the feet, and about six hammer dents.
- Matte stone that never glows.

**Mark:** the cloud with `>_` from `engraving/codex.svg`, exact. The knot in `/tmp/codex-official-icon.png` is a
different mark.
- At a uniform scale of about 0.9 the cloud is about 0.24 m.
- Centre it on the +Z cheek at y 0.94 m, with the plaque flush with the iron.
- A 1 h chamfered frame makes it read as stamped.
- The cut is 1.5 cm deep.

**Runes:** the shared Forge set, cut 1.5 cm deep, with 2 cm strokes.
- Seven glyphs of 1 × 1.25 cells across the +Z face plate, centred over the mark.
- Five on −Z, three shrinking along each horn flank, and one under the hardy hole.
- Order them by distance from the mark.

## Dragon chimney

The envelope is 24 × 16 × 18 cells, with the mount at [12,0,9] and the nostril emitter at [0,2,9].

**Why the current one fails:**
- It is one value, in its roof's own material (Tide is slate on slate).
- Its wings are upright fins, taller than the stack.
- Its head is a box with no neck dip, and the 1-cell horns and tail vanish.

**Pose: asleep like a cat.**
- The crooked stack rises to 13–14 cells with a lip and a dark open mouth. It is the tallest shape.
- The body coils around the base of the stack at 0–6 cells, with a shoulder hump at 7.
- The neck dips to 3, then the long wedge head rests on two crossed paws.
- The horns sweep back to 8 cells, 2 cells thick at the root.
- One wing folds flat along the far flank as a triangle that peaks at 9.
- The tail wraps the front, and its spade tip curls over the nose.

**Reading at 30 m** (about 35 px on a phone):
- A serrated spine of five plates, shrinking toward the tail.
- Horns are the brightest small shape.
- The back sits two value steps off its kit's roof:
  - Hearth: brick, with limestone for belly and horns.
  - Tide: darkStone, with slate plates and a cream belly.
  - Grove: moss, with a cream belly and goldLeaf horns.
- A light belly band edges the coil where it meets the roof.
- Air shows under the chin, between the paws and inside the tail loop.
- It must pass in greyscale.

**Motion** (this needs a seam from main):
- Smoke breathes on a 4.5 s cycle, and the ribs swell 1.5% with it.
- The tail tip twitches about every 20 s.
- At the strike, one lid opens to a warm slit for 1.5 s, a long exhale lets out a triple puff,
  then it settles.

## Gasp, not fine

- Near-black iron with one living ember inside the exact mark.
- Heat that visibly flows out into the runes, then drains in order.
- Real weight.
- A dragon you read in one second, tucked in like a cat.

## Five common failures

1. **The badge:** the mark is proud, shows a seam or a value change, or is redrawn.
2. **Glow everywhere:** white-hot cuts, runes lit when cold, a halo, or heat in the stone.
3. **The blob:** the dragon matches its roof, has fin wings and no neck dip, and its horns vanish.
4. **Noise for craft:** speckle everywhere, or perfect boxes and a cone horn.
5. **The toy anvil:** a short horn, no arches, a decal hole, a puny block, or a giant logo.
