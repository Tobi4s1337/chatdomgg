# GM10 — MashaD4, a kingdom of its own

MashaD4 (154972160) opens on its own composed mountain city instead of the shared settlement compiler's grid.
Code: `apps/web/src/genesis/kingdoms/masha/` (land, city, seat, composition, capture, tests) and
`packages/content/src/genesis-kingdoms/masha/` (roof choice, The Copper Kettle). The only shared edit is the
registry hook in `apps/web/src/genesis/index.tsx`: a channel with its own composition supplies its land, plan,
seat, style, camera and opening hour; every other channel keeps the shared compiler.

## Decisions

- **The seeded candidate, deepened, never moved.** The horns stand on the seed's own ridge centres, the pass keeps
  its saddle, the keep keeps Crown Hill. New mountains (shoulders, flanks, a crown of four summits and a far
  ridge) are labelled seeded draws of the landscape's own key, so every visitor sees the same land.
- **The sea stands 340 m further off.** Genesis ground may rise only 0.22 m per metre from the shore. On the seed's
  coast the horns and the saddle were cut flat at about 135 m: the pass vanished into a table wall. The shoreline,
  its islands and the river mouth keep their seeded shapes and move out together, leaving river meadows below
  the city and room for real mountains (horns 184/194 m, the pass notched at 150.5 m, summits to 257 m).
- **The river springs below the city** (seed point 6) and its remaining bends stretch to the moved mouth with a
  seeded meander between each. A stream carving its bank through a mountain left a cliff where the bank
  envelope ends, and its old course split the lower terraces.
- **Terraces are rounded squares, not circles** (superellipse n = 4 about the keep). Homes stand on whole quarter
  turns and a row only closes up along a straight lane; circular lanes wasted half their frontage. The terraces
  wrap the mount past the keep's sides until the box's north edge, one row of homes each, every home facing out
  across its own lane (homes have no back windows). Pitch 25 m, lanes 5 m, grade at most 10 %, cut at most
  7 m behind a lot (a terrace bank).
- **The city fits the view's detail box.** The kingdom view draws detailed ground for at most 512 m a side and
  throws beyond it. The city is a 444 × 445 m box about the keep (bounds 477 × 476 m, chunk-aligned 496 m);
  the Pass Road climbs on beyond it to the saddle, where the distant terrain renders the true surface.
- **Fixed places on the land.** The land levels the pass terrace (crown tower), the cathedral close and the inn
  yard as plateaus of their own natural height; Bell Court sits 391 m from the keep on the stair's axis. City and
  land read the same constants (`MASHA_LAND`), so they cannot drift apart. Courts without homes do not grade the
  ground, which is why these are land features rather than courts.
- **Eight wards of about 64 homes.** The stair halves each terrace; halves are grouped in slot order. Old Row is
  the first terrace under the castle wall, and slots count outward, so the earliest addresses are nearest the keep.
- **Roofs from the colours.** OKLab distance from each kit's roof material to the profile colour targets
  (weighted 1, .6, .45, .35) chooses slate, so the city is the tide kit. The recipe itself is not rewritten.
- **The cathedral site fronts Bell Court** (beside it, on the levelled close) instead of a terrace gap: its
  34 m depth cannot sit inside a 25 m terrace without cutting the lane above.
- **The crown tower's watch fire is the beacon**, the one fire in the notch between the horns at dusk. Braziers
  render open flames, so they are used only for true fires (the keep's court braziers and the watch fire).
- **The arms are proposals.** Two standards at the foot of the Crown Stair and the crest monument beside them,
  in the snapped profile colours (`composeGenesisHeraldry`).
- **Pip's lamp posts stand unlit** at every landing of the Crown Stair: a lamp burns only when a keeper lights it.
- **Emote art is honest absence.** The channel has no approved, channel-bound emote derivative; the composer
  returns no monument and no vane, and the glass claims nothing.
- **The Copper Kettle faces south from its yard** at Bell Court's south-east corner (the land's levelled `inn-yard`,
  a court of its own so nothing else claims it), door and kettle sign to the road up from the shore like every home
  in the city, not west onto the court: from the road in, the inn is the first lit house a traveller reaches. Its
  lanterns hang unlit and its door stays shut until a keeper runtime keeps it.
- **Keepers stand at their own places:** Tansy at The Copper Kettle's door, Pip at the foot of the Crown Stair, where
  the lamp round starts.
- **The hero view stands in the river meadows**, 680 m south of the keep and 100 m up, so Bell Court, the inn, the
  arms and the cathedral close sit in the lower third above the caption, the terraces fill the middle and the keep,
  the pass and its crown tower stand against the dusk sky. Closer in (560 m) the court and the inn fell off the
  frame's bottom edge.
- **Opening hour 18.9** is set in the address when it names none, so every view and capture shares it.

## needs (main)

- `needs:` mount the land's sound in the potential kingdom view: `createGenesisSoundscape(kingdomAudioHost(audio),
  composed.sound)` inside `createKingdomSound`'s graph (so the sound button and visibility mute apply), updated each
  frame with `{ realmMs, hour, listener: camera }`; export `./genesis-biomes` from `@chatdom/audio`. MashaD4's
  composition already hands the exact input (`composeMashaKingdom(...).sound`, tested against
  `describeGenesisSoundscape`).
- `needs:` an environment/grade hook on `PotentialKingdomView` (sky, sun and fog tint per kingdom), so a kingdom can
  carry its own dusk light; today the light is `getEnvironment(hour)` for every kingdom and the only lever is the hour.
- `needs:` a keeper runtime in the potential view (Tansy at The Copper Kettle, Pip on the Crown Stair, lighting the
  lamps at dusk); `composeMashaKingdom(...).keepers` carries the proposals and their sites.
- `needs:` approved, channel-bound emote derivatives for 154972160 (candidates by shape: lovaChess, lovaHeart,
  lovaStar for glass and the monument); the composition picks them up through `composeGenesisEmoteArt`.
- `needs:` a lamp-light seam for unlit fixtures (a small warm point light, not a flame), so keepers' lamps can burn.
