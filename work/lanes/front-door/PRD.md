## The part
chatdom.io becomes a front page you would send to a friend: a short story told in real pictures of the kingdom, then the live Atlas of Twitch.

What is there today (checked on Sun 1 Nov 2026 at 22:40): `/` is one parchment plate over one static picture of Willowmere ("Your chat has a home", sign in or look around), with a link to `/showcase`. `/showcase` is seven text cards, five stamped SIMULATED, linking to fixture benches (a made-up citizen called Wren, a simulated night), plus three Genesis kingdoms whose streamers have not given consent (L11). `/map` is the Atlas (atlas-map's). `/robots.txt`, `/privacy`, `/imprint` and `/sitemap.xml` all return the front door's HTML with status 404. Every response carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, added by the Traefik route in front of the gateway, not by our code. The CSP is strict and same-origin (`default-src 'self'`, fonts from `'self'` only, images also from `static-cdn.jtvnw.net`). Sign-in asks Twitch for `openid` only and fails at Twitch today because the redirect URI is not registered yet (L01, Tobias). There is no Twitch mark and no "not affiliated" line anywhere. The kit's fonts are Latin only.

The end state:
- **The story.** A first-time visitor sees, in five to seven full-bleed sections of at most about 15 words each: the kingdom at dusk as the hero (real captures of the grand land, which is every kingdom's land from now on), presence as light (a window lights when a name appears in chat), the Forge turning words into a thing, a hamlet growing into a city, Twitch both ways (sub, cheer, hype train, raid), then the live Atlas full-bleed with "Explore the map". "How it works" is told in four pictures with at most 40 words each, plus "what we never collect" and the privacy link.
- **Sign in with Twitch from anywhere.** A sign-in seal on every page we own (and, through the kit, on the Atlas), with the official Twitch Glitch, unaltered, set in a painted brass frame. Signed out, everything is browsable and designed. After sign-in you come back to exactly where you were.
- **The legal pages.** An imprint, a privacy policy and terms for saartech.de, written from the real operator details and from what the code really does, good enough for German law and for Twitch's extension review, linked from every page. Every public page says "Live from Twitch. Chatdom is not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch."
- **Every name renders.** Names in Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Thai, Chinese, Japanese and Korean render in our own self-hosted faces, and a Latin-only page never downloads a CJK font.
- **Honest everywhere.** No SIMULATED label on any public page. Fixtures and staged benches are not reachable on the public origin as if they were real.
- **Ready to be found, on one switch.** `robots.txt` and the sitemap are real files, and one gateway setting decides whether the site may be indexed. It stays closed until Tobias opens it at launch.
- **/showcase** is folded into the story.

The bar: it is beautiful and game-like, made of the carved kit (wood, brass, parchment, wax, hand-lettered headings), not a SaaS landing page. It reads in seconds, paints at once and is designed on a phone, not shrunk. It looks right on Tobias's real GPU.

## Decisions
1. **Story first, then the live Atlas** (decision 17). The story page is the front door for signed-out visitors; the Atlas is the page's last section and the place everything leads to.
2. **The front page stays its own small build** (today's `door` Vite config, moved or extended into `apps/web/src/site/`), so first paint never waits for the kingdom engine. The Atlas embed and the 3D hero load lazily, after first paint, from their own chunks.
3. **Pictures are real.** Live 3D and real engine captures first; generated art only for ornament or ambience (decision 21). A staged scene shot in the engine is fine as long as no caption claims it was a real event, no real streamer appears without consent, and no viewer count is shown. Captions name the kingdom and the date it was drawn.
4. **Who is featured.** Only tobi4s_'s kingdom (Willowmere) and the grand land seeded for tobi4s_, until Tobias records consent for The_AGGman, PinkKittyRose and MashaD4 (L11, his). Their kingdoms leave the public pages with `/showcase`.
5. **/showcase folds into the story.** It answers `301` to `/`. Its links to fixture benches go away. The fixture-only citizen card bench leaves the public page list; the avatar builder (a real tool) and `/-/grand` (real generation) stay.
6. **No SIMULATED labels anywhere** (decision 25, overriding AGENTS.md and the ralph-lane rule). Public pages show real data or nothing. The public gateway refuses page URLs that ask for staged data, and refuses to start in simulated sign-in mode on an `https` origin.
7. **One indexing switch.** `PUBLIC_INDEXING=closed|open` in the gateway, default `closed`. Closed: `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` on every response, `robots.txt` disallows everything, the sitemap is empty. Open: no `X-Robots-Tag` on pages, `robots.txt` allows the product pages and disallows `/api/`, `/live-realm-data/`, `/map-data/`, `/presence-data/`, `/forge-data/`, `/internal/` and `/-/`, and the sitemap lists `/`, `/map`, `/how-it-works` and the legal pages. Traefik's own noindex header stays until Tobias removes it; the lane never edits Traefik.
8. **The Twitch mark** is the official Glitch, unaltered (decision 20), in the colours and clear space Twitch's brand guidelines allow. The painted brass frame sits around the clear space, never on the mark. The button text is "Sign in with Twitch". The guideline version you checked is recorded in `twitch-brand.md`.
9. **Sign-in asks only for `openid`** (Atlas §3); more scopes are asked for at the moment of use, by whoever needs them. The sign-in service accepts only plain paths as `returnTo` (no queries or fragments; `apps/api/src/realm-auth/service.ts`, main's), so the view travels in `sessionStorage` under one key written just before leaving and read once on return. If a page path is refused by `returnTo` (for example `/how-it-works`, which has hyphens), return to `/` and restore from the stored view, or ask main with a `needs:` line; don't edit realm-auth.
10. **The legal pages** are English (decision 32), prerendered static HTML in the kit's look, at `/imprint`, `/privacy` and `/terms`, linked from every page we own and offered to the Atlas and the panel through the kit. The footer link reads "Imprint · Impressum" so German visitors find it. Review notes live only in `legal-review.md`, never on the page.
11. **The privacy policy describes what the code does today, not the vision.** Before drafting, inventory the real data flows from the code and the running services, and write the inventory into `legal-review.md`: Twitch sign-in (user id, public display name, the session cookie and the 10-minute sign-in cookie, sessions in Postgres); chat presence in founded kingdoms (names lit in windows, lurkers as unnamed lights whose presence accrues privately; Tech §7.1) and that chat logs are not stored (check it); the panel extension's identity share; the Atlas's public channel data from Twitch (names, pictures, languages, games, live state) and pictures loaded from Twitch's image CDN (the visitor's browser connects to Twitch for them); what the Forge sends to its generation service and which provider that is (read the Forge app; if words reach a third party, it must be named, with the transfer basis); any speech provider only if it is live on the public origin; browser storage used (session storage for reconnect seats; necessary, so no consent banner under § 25 (2) TDDDG); no analytics and no advertising (decision 42); self-hosted fonts; hosting (the host is a Hetzner dedicated server: confirm the data-centre location with Tobias); retention; the GDPR rights and the Saarland authority. Do not promise features that are not built (`!chatdom forget`, data export and the self-serve hide are later goals): say how to ask by email today, and note in `legal-review.md` where the text must change when they ship.
12. **The terms** cover the website and the panel extension: who runs it; that it is free to use; that a Twitch account is needed for some parts and Twitch's own terms apply there; the rules for what people make (pointing to the channel's moderators and Twitch's rules, Safety §1); how to report content and the contact point for users and authorities (EU Digital Services Act); a licence for what people make so it can be shown in the kingdom; availability without guarantee; liability as German law allows for a free service (unlimited for intent, gross negligence and harm to life, body or health; otherwise limited); governing law Germany without taking away a consumer's mandatory home protections; changes to the terms. No Bits or paid features are promised.
13. **The imprint** follows § 5 DDG and § 18 (2) MStV with the operator details above. Open points for Tobias go into `legal-review.md`: a second fast way to reach the operator besides email (a contact form or phone; German law expects direct, fast contact), the VAT ID if one exists, and whether a Chatdom-specific contact address should replace tk@saartech.de.
14. **Fonts are ours to serve.** Every face is self-hosted and sliced by script; nothing loads from a font CDN.
15. **The live gateway is never restarted by this lane.** Changes reach chatdom.io when Tobias or main rebuilds `worktrees/public` and restarts it. Write what the deploy needs (new settings, the Traefik change) as `needs:` lines.

## How to work
- **Goals, not tasks.** Work FD10 to FD13 in order. A goal can take many sessions: leave everything building and working, write `next: <id> <what is done, what remains>` as your last progress line, and stop. Tick a goal only when its done-when is truly met. FD10 comes first and must be reviewed and checkpointed by Monday morning; if time runs short, land the story page, sign-in, the legal pages and the honest public origin before any polish.
- **Never run git.** No commit, merge, rebase, reset, stash or push. A reviewer commits your work after each session. Leave shas out of progress lines.
- **Progress lines** go to `work/lanes/front-door/progress.txt`, one per session: `<id>: <what you did, decisions, doubts>` and, when unfinished, `next: <id> ...`. Use `needs: <what>` for anything outside the scope (Traefik, realm-auth, a shared contract, another lane's mount) and keep going on the rest. Use `waiting: <id> needs <what>` only when a goal truly cannot move.
- **Look at it.** Every visual change is checked with screenshots you look at, at 1440x900 and 390x844 (`.agents/skills/look/SKILL.md`; `scripts/public/look.mjs` takes one still under the shared capture lock). Show a before and after of the same view. Evidence goes in `work/evidence/front-door/<goal>/`, PNGs under about 2 MB.
- **Tobias's real GPU.** His Chrome on an RTX 5080 is reached through `chatdom-pc-lease` (read its header first). One lane at a time, at most 10 minutes: inside the lease, serve your own build on `127.0.0.1:5192` (a second server, such as your own gateway's realm, on 5191), wait for it, then `chatdom-pc shot http://localhost:5192/<path> <out.png> --size 1440x900`, or `pc fps` for frame timing. Always the full URL. Exit 3 means his PC is off: take one short SwiftShader still under the capture lock instead (hold it under 5 minutes), and never treat SwiftShader numbers as performance evidence. Use several short leases for a sequence of hero frames rather than one long one.
- **Your own servers.** Outside a lease, run test servers only on a free port in 5170 to 5186 or 5198 to 5199, with `--strictPort`, write the port in progress, and stop them when the test ends. Never use 5187 or 5190 to 5197. For the sign-in test, migrate and use your own throwaway schema (`apps/realm/scripts/migrate.mjs --schema <yours>`), never the live schema, and point the gateway at the live realm only for read-only stills (`PUBLIC_REALM_TARGET=http://127.0.0.1:5197`).
- **The live chatdom.io gateway is never restarted, rebuilt or stopped by this lane**, and neither are the containers, Traefik routes, the Atlas data service or main's preview.
- **The host is shared** (12 cores, with Tobias's other services). Run builds and encodes with `nice -n 10`. Run the tests for the files you touched (`pnpm vitest run apps/api/src/public packages/ui/src apps/web/src/site ...`) and at most one niced root typecheck per session; never the full suite, never `pnpm verify`. No long recordings on the CPU.
- **Secrets.** Never print or read the values in `~/.config/chatdom/*.env`, the repo's `.env` or any token store; pass env files with `--env-file` or `PUBLIC_ENV_FILE` without echoing them.
- **The seams** are in `SEAMS.md`: the Atlas embed you consume from atlas-map, the hero capture and later the live hero you need from grand-world, and the kit you own for everyone else. Never edit another lane's files to make a seam work; write it in SEAMS.md and as a `needs:` line, build your side so it works with or without theirs, and keep going.
- **Words in the repo.** Plain English. No tool, model or orchestration names, no thread ids. Product words only: talk about the product and its quality.
- **Sub-helpers** may take a folder each (a batch of pictures, the font slicing, a test sweep) inside the scope; review what they produce yourself, and they never run git.

## Round notes (settled across all eight lanes; these win over anything above)

- **Lanes this round:** grand-world, play, forge-wish, front-door, atlas-map, raid-show, panel-window, teaser. They all run at the same time. Each one owns only its own scope. Their reviewed checkpoints are merged together continuously into one integration branch, and that branch becomes the release on Monday.
- **Realm migrations:** each lane has its own number. raid-show `0011`, panel-window `0012`, play `0013`, forge-wish `0014`, grand-world `0015`. A lane that needs a second one appends a letter, for example `0012b`. If the migration runner needs contiguous numbers, the numbers are renumbered when the lanes are merged; never renumber another lane's file.
- **Raid roads:** pull, not push. raid-show owns the durable, append-only feed of witnessed raids, `GET /realm/raid-roads?after=&limit=`, with items that follow `AtlasRaidSchema` (`packages/shared/src/atlas-raids.ts`). atlas-map pulls from that feed with a cursor into its own road ledger, so it can catch up after a restart. atlas-map's `POST /v1/roads/observe` is optional and raid-show does not depend on it.
- **Contracts:** adding to `packages/shared` is allowed for every lane: new files and new optional fields, but no changed or removed exports. The realm modules for your own feature are yours (Forge modules for forge-wish, raid-gate for raid-show, the panel ingress for panel-window, look storage for play). Hooks into the realm core (rooms, lifecycle, server.ts, main.ts) are small, additive and typed, written up in your SEAMS.md. "Widen the contract" is not something to wait on main for.
- **Shared allowlists and configs:** `apps/api/src/public/routes.ts` (the public gateway allowlist, owned by front-door), `apps/web/src/main.tsx`, `apps/web/vite.config.ts` and package.json scripts take one additive line per route or registration from any lane. No reformatting.
- **SIMULATED:** front-door stops the shared kit from rendering any SIMULATED chip but keeps every exported name. Each lane removes the chips and fixtures in its own scope and updates its own tests. Leftovers are fixed when the lanes are merged.
- **Real GPU:** `chatdom-pc-lease` (its header is the manual) and `chatdom-pc shot|fps` with full `http://localhost:5192/...` URLs. Each lease is at most 10 minutes. Render long sequences in chunks across several leases.
- **Engine:** a lane run by an engine that commits its own work commits each checkpoint on its lane branch as `.agents/skills/ralph-lane/SKILL.md` says. A lane whose worker is told "never run git" leaves the commits to its reviewer. Both are fine, and only the instruction you were given applies to you.
