# LT11 — Every Twitch event shapes the kingdom, and it keeps your place between streams

Working notes for the whole part: the design calls, what is built, what is left. Read with `PRD.md` and
`progress.txt`. Vision: `chatdom/Twitch.md` §3, §4 (follows, coming home), §5 (subs, address), §7 (going live),
§8 (kingdom rewards, polls), §13 (decrees); `chatdom/Moments.md` §6.

## The path (same as LT10)

Twitch (EventSub, or Twitch CLI mock through the signed door) → Herald `ChatIngest` → durable realm outbox
(`moment` lane, ahead of presence) → realm ingress `acceptMoment` / `accept` → town records + homes + public moment
facts → `/api/realm/watch/:kingdom` → `/stream?source=realm&kingdom=129634568`.

## Decisions

1. **Follow → a home on the edge of town, with a sapling.** `channel.follow` v2 (broadcaster as moderator,
   `moderator:read:followers`). The realm records the follow date as signed tenure evidence, settles the follower
   (their public name, then `allocate` with that evidence: a follow made now lands at the growing edge of the newest
   quarter, i.e. the edge of town) and plants a sapling by their door. Someone who already has a home keeps it:
   nobody moves once settled, a re-follow only plants the sapling if it is not there. Public: a `follow` fact for the
   beat (credit, home slot) and the lasting `grove`.
2. **Sub → the banner, and the tenure street.** The banner beat already exists (support notices → `subscription`
   facts → home banner). New: a subscriber with no home is settled before the banner, with their sub months as
   evidence (one street toward the castle within their quarter, `choosePlot`), so the banner unfurls on their new home
   on their tenure street. (No follower record is kept to combine with it: a sub settles by its months alone.) The entitlement notice carries
   the public name Twitch showed (optional field, additive). Months count once, at settling (Twitch.md §5).
3. **Kingdom rewards (channel points).** Chatdom's starter rewards are recognised by title, exactly:
   `Ring the Bell`, `Call a Festival`, `Plant a Tree`, `Show My Home`. Title match (not a stored reward id) is
   deliberate: the Twitch CLI mock and a reward the monarch makes by hand in the Creator Dashboard work the same, and
   the realm stays stateless about Twitch reward ids. Herald keeps no user token (by design, LT2), so nothing here
   creates rewards or polls on Twitch: the monarch makes them, the scripts' `--live` mode waits for the real act. Other rewards are
   the streamer's own: nothing acts on them. Redeeming is an act in this kingdom, so a redeemer with no home is
   settled first when the reward needs a home (Plant a Tree needs a plot; Show My Home needs a home); the bell and
   the festival need none. The realm marks nothing on Twitch: the redemption stays in the streamer's queue.
   - Ring the Bell: the town looks up, the bell rings from the castle.
   - Call a Festival: ten minutes of festival in the streets, hung in the town's decreed colours.
   - Plant a Tree: a tree with your name on your plot. Lasting (the grove).
   - Show My Home: the director visits your home, its window flares and the neighbours' answer.
4. **Polls → the town decides.** Every Twitch poll stands as a decree board in the plaza while it runs: the question,
   each choice a pennant on its pole, rising with its share. One person, one vote: channel-point votes are taken off
   (Twitch.md §8). When it ends the winner's pennant tops its pole. A poll whose every choice is one of the town's
   colours (`Ember`, `Gold`, `Rose`, `Tide`, `Meadow`, `Dusk`, `Snow`, `Night`) is a **decree the kingdom carries out**:
   the town flies the winning colours on every bunting run and on the gate's standard from then on, across streams,
   until the next colours decree. A tie decides nothing ("the town is divided"). Any other poll is shown with its
   result and nothing acts on it. A poll Twitch moderated is set aside. On tobi4s_ the monarch starts the poll
   (Ember / Tide / Meadow); `poll.mjs --live` waits for its result.
5. **Going live opens the gate and calls people back.** `stream.online` (type `live`) opens a night: the realm keeps a
   night record (number, stream id; a second notice for the same stream is the same night). Public `night` fact
   (current): the gate's braziers catch, the gate's standard rises in the town's colours, the beacon catches on the
   castle, caption "The gate opens · Night N · welcome home, everyone". `stream.offline` rests the night.
   **Welcome back:** while a night is open, the first chat of a citizen whose home was settled before tonight plays
   the relight at their own home (their window flares warm, the street's lamps answer) with "Welcome back, ‹name›"
   (and "home after N days" from 14 days away, Twitch.md §4). Once per person per night. Their home is the one they
   left: the deed never moves. Many coming home at once take their turns on the stage like any beat (bounded queue);
   one who joins after the queue is full is still welcomed (recorded), just without the relight.
   Someone settled tonight (a fresh home from chat, a follow, a sub) is marked home already: no relight for a home
   that did not stand before tonight.
6. **Lasting state is one current fact each**, keyed so a newer one replaces the older in the public checkpoint:
   `night` (key `night`), `grove` (key `grove`, every planted sapling and tree by home slot, bounded 4096), `decree`
   (key `decree.colours`). A reconnect or a new stream restores them without replaying their beats.
7. **Origin.** Everything from the bench door is SIMULATED; a LIVE realm refuses it. Real on tobi4s_: follows
   (anyone can follow), the night (going live), polls/decrees and rewards (made by the monarch on Twitch) once the
   broadcaster grants `moderator:read:followers`, `channel:manage:redemptions` and `channel:manage:polls`; subs
   need a real subscriber.
8. **Where it lives.** Realm: `apps/realm/src/town/` (records, `applyTown`, `applyHomecoming`), wired in
   `moments/service.ts` (settle before the record transaction, through `TownSettler` = `createRealmCitizens.settle`,
   which signs the act's own tenure evidence) and `evening/citizens.ts` (the homecoming hook after a chat
   observation, outside the writer). Web: `twitch-moments/town.ts` (frame types, timings, dyes), `town-beat.ts`
   (state + beats + captions + cues + stage), `town-view.ts`/`town-art.ts` (grove, relight, gate, bell),
   `decree-view.ts`/`decree-art.ts` (board, colours, festival).

## Integration review — 2026-11-01

The additive shared contracts were approved by main for integration. Outbound moment budget is 288 KiB: the maximum
4,096-entry grove with two maximum epoch stamps per home is about 271 KiB including the public message envelope.
The runtime budget regression restores all entries through the public projection and browser controller.

Correctness fixes made in review:

- One redemption is claimed by its redemption ID as well as its delivery ID, in the same transaction as its facts.
  Unknown statuses do nothing; allocation failure leaves the act available for Herald to retry.
- A closed broadcast cannot reopen, older online evidence cannot replace a newer night, and chat from before the
  current broadcast cannot become a homecoming. Accepted Get Streams transitions restore night state at startup or
  after a missed event; a broadcast older than the opening window restores quietly. Repeated minute reads add no acts.
- Older polls cannot reclaim the board or overwrite a newer colours decree. Unknown terminal statuses are invalid.
  The browser retains only one running poll and releases its current entry when it ends.
- Follow tenure evidence records its actual `eventsub.follow` source. The pre-existing Helix source remains accepted.
- Homecoming caches contain committed state only. The citizen test fixture now handles main's actual growth binding.

No live Twitch writes, service restarts or new captures were performed for this review. Scoped tests and lint are
reported in `progress.txt`; main owns the final commit and integration checks.

Visual review of the nine existing LT11 images found `decree@1920x1080.png` is only the loading screen: reject it as
evidence. The poll image is a distant town shot and does not demonstrate the board. The sub image has a caption but
no cloth because that native house supplies no legal banner wall anchor. The grove renderer retains 4,096 records but
shows a fixed first 512 plantings, so large-town visual coverage needs a view-based selection policy. These are
explicit review doubts, not passing visual acceptance.
