## The part
Walking the kingdom with your chat is a joy. You sign in, your citizen steps out of your own front door, and you are in the town with the people from chat.
- **It moves like a good game:** smooth, responsive movement with weight, a camera that follows kindly, stairs, slopes and doors that just work, no snapping and no sliding feet.
- **You look like you:** a full-body wardrobe (hats, cloaks, tunics, boots, tools, the earned keepsakes), dressed in the builder and worn in the street, readable from the stream's distance.
- **You can say something without typing:** gestures and emotes (wave, bow, cheer, dance, sit by the fire, point), chat bubbles over heads and names that stay readable without cluttering.
- **Others feel present:** everyone else's avatar moves naturally, smoothed over the network, turning, idling and greeting, never teleporting; a crowd gathers and parts around you.
- **You are on stream:** when you walk, the stream view can find you, so the streamer and the whole chat see you arrive at the square.
It holds at the bar of the window wave and the Forge, runs smoothly on the stream view and on a phone, and everything that exists keeps working with it (homes and growth, the Forge, the moments, the raid road). Show it as two or three people walking together through the town, in stills and short clips you have watched. Fan out sub-agents (terra high for wardrobe pieces and animation, one folder each). You own it end to end and make the design calls.
Captures: take the shared capture lock for one still or one short clip at a time and release it in between; while you wait for it, keep building, and never end a turn blocked on it.
