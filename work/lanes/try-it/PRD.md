## The part
Anyone on Twitch can try it.
- **Inside Twitch:** the panel extension (your citizen card, your raid passport, your home) runs on twitch.tv/tobi4s_ in Hosted Test. Build the uploadable zip, check it against Twitch's review rules (CSP, no remote code, the Extension Helper, sizes), and write the exact console steps the channel owner clicks on dev.twitch.tv: create or pick the extension version, the asset hosting settings, the allowlists and URLs, uploading the zip, moving to Hosted Test, installing and activating it on the channel. Leave them in `deploy/public/EXTENSION-STEPS.md`, short and numbered, so they can be emailed and followed without questions.
- **On the web:** a stable public entry at https://chatdom.io where anyone signs in with Twitch and walks into a kingdom, with the showcase one click away. It works for a first-time visitor with no setup: sign in, get your citizen, walk; or just look around without signing in. It survives many visitors at once and a restart without losing anyone's home.
The bar: a stranger who has never heard of Chatdom opens chatdom.io, signs in with Twitch and is walking in a lit kingdom within a minute, and a viewer on tobi4s_ opens the panel and sees their own card and passport. Nothing private leaks (tokens, ids beyond what Twitch shows publicly), and the page reads like a product, not a dev build.
You own it end to end and make the design calls.
Context: the panel app in `apps/panel/` (`Panel.tsx`, `card/`, `passport/`, `adapters/`, `helper.ts`, `panel.html`, `config.html`, `live_config.html`); Twitch sign-in in `apps/api/src/realm-auth/` and `apps/api/src/main.ts`; the web entry in `apps/web/src/showcase/`, `kingdom-entry/`, `realm/`; how the running services are built and started today: web (vite preview), API, realm, herald and the Forge runner, each from `apps/<name>` with `dist/` builds.
Hosting: the public host, its route and its certificate are provided for you, not made from your sandbox. Make a single production build and start command that serves the web app, the API and the realm behind one origin (one port, the realm's realtime included), plus a short `deploy/public/README.md`: the build command, the start command, the port, the environment variables it reads (names only, never values), and the Twitch redirect URI to register for https://chatdom.io. Then write it as an exact `needs:` (`needs: public host: <command>, port <n>, redirect <uri>`) and keep going; it gets wired up and the answer lands in your progress.
Boundaries: the kingdom scene, renderer and stream directors (`apps/web/src/kingdom/**`, `apps/web/src/stream/**`, `packages/render/**`), `packages/shared` contracts, the herald and the realm's world logic are main's: read them freely; ask for any seam as an exact `needs:` with the patch written out. Never touch the running services on ports 5190–5199.
Work in checkpoints: if a session ends before the part is done, leave everything building and working, write `next: <id> <what is done, what remains>` and carry on next session. For visual changes, show a before/after of the same view. Hand main exact `needs:` for any seam outside scope and keep going.
Captures: take the shared capture lock for one still or one short clip at a time and release it in between, so main can capture between your frames; never hold it for a whole series.
