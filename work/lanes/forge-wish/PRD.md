## The part

Words almost always become things. A citizen walking in their kingdom comes to the anvil, or opens the Forge from the slot play's HUD offers, types a wish in their own words, and watches it being forged where they stand: the accepted ritual, the reveal, the piece flying home and settling on its slot in the kingdom's own style. When the Forge can't make exactly that, it makes the closest thing it can and says so privately and kindly. It refuses only what is unsafe. It remembers every piece and who wished for it. It never loses a wish, even when a service restarts mid-wish. It is quick and cheap enough to run all evening. Then its range grows from three roof parts to every house slot, composed parts, things for the home, and things to wear.

### Why wishes fail today (found 1 Nov from `.forge.local/runs.jsonl` and the diagnostics beside it)

- Since 31 Oct, 15 wishes ran end to end: 3 approved, 12 refused. Every refusal came from the result gate, after the piece was built. The word gate passed all 15, so the safety floor was never the problem.
- 11 of the 12 refusals were for wishes the kit was made for: 6 dragon chimneys (0 approved), 4 moon lanterns (0 approved), owl weathervanes (3 of 7 approved). The only wish outside the kit, "a dog painting", was drafted as a scallop-shell weathervane titled "The Tidebound Dog" and refused, as the policy intends.
- 11 refusals cite `unfaithful_subject`, 1 cites `unreadable_marks`. These are quality questions, but `policy.ts` puts them in the same list as the safety floor, so a quality failure becomes a safety-style `refuse`: it plays the public mood beat and tells the maker "The forge cannot shape this wish".
- One read can veto alone. The low-effort Luna read gives a self-estimated probability per question, and anything above 0.85 refuses no matter what Terra says. The same owl plan (copper, no accent) passed on 31 Oct at 00:31 and was refused at 11:26, 12:00 and 20:29 with Luna at 0.97 to 0.99 while Terra passed it every time. Terra's own `refuse` also vetoes alone.
- The image the result gate sees is not the piece people see. `contact-sheet.ts` draws six flat, unlit, orthographic 232×184 tiles of the 32³ part box, including the host roof and socket around it, plus one small shaded view. A fresh vision read of the dragon chimney's sheet (`image-gate-diagnostic.json`) described "a central grey-blue block, a dark square cap and a long beige post… no sleeping dragon is visibly recognizable". The authored dragon does not survive that drawing.
- There is no retry after the result gate. `run.ts` only redrafts when assembly fails; one bad read ends the wish.
- The vocabulary is 3 slots × 7 motifs × 3 finishes × 4 accents, and both the draft prompt and the judge are told that an unrelated motif must not pass. So any wish outside the 7 motifs is refused by design. Softening was never built.
- Each wish makes 5 cold `codex exec` runs (2 word reads, 1 draft, 2 result reads; 6 when a draft fails to assemble), each in a fresh temporary home with no daemon, so every call pays the full start-up cost.
- Wishes live only in memory: the bench service on 5195 restarts with every main build (10 to 90 minutes apart) and the realm's evening bridge keeps pending wishes in a map, so a restart loses wishes in flight. The bench accepts one wish at a time.
- In the live kingdom the anvil only says "Write !forge … in Twitch chat"; typing a wish works only on the bench page. No realm has ever committed a forged piece (there is no `forge-approvals` record on this host).

## Decisions

- **2026-11-01 — FW10 policy checkpoint.** Safety and quality are separate outputs. A refusal requires two independent reads identifying the same floor concern; a disagreement holds privately. Recognition, completeness and stray marks become bounded quality scores, with a deterministic weighted aggregate (60/25/15), never safety vetoes. Both raw dimension reads and the aggregate are private, policy-versioned evidence. Missing quality scores become zero; missing safety still cannot approve. This checkpoint does not certify recognition, live-model repeatability or delivery: the quiet result retry, explicit softening and the real-wish proof remain FW10 work.
- **2026-11-01 — Tobias's answers win.** Decision 6 (soften and quietly retry; only safety refuses) supersedes the forge-pieces note in `work/HANDOFF-LANES.md` that kept judge behaviour closed, and Tech §11.3's "a failed part retries once, then the Forge refuses gracefully" for quality failures. Decision 7 widens the range to all slots, composed parts and wardrobe items. Decision 24: choose at the reveal, plus a worn twin. Decision 25: no SIMULATED labels.
- **2026-11-01 — Two gates stay; fewer calls come from escalation.** Every wish still passes an independent word gate before drafting and an independent result gate after assembly, outside the drafting run (Safety §4.2). Calls drop because each gate starts with one read and escalates only when unsure or about to refuse, as Tech §11.3 already describes for images. Safety is never traded for speed.
- **2026-11-01 — Keep the Codex login for the Forge (decision 8).** No API key path. The consumer-terms question and the shared rate limit stay open for Tobias (`context/open-questions.md`). - **2026-11-01 — Coal comes only from presence.** One coal per piece that lands. Quiet retries and softening are free; a refusal, a hold or a cold forge never spends one. Money never buys a wish, a retry or a place in the queue (ChatDom §8, decision 27).

## How to work

- **Goals, not tasks.** A goal can take several sessions. Work in checkpoints: leave everything building and working, then write `next: FW10 <what is done / what remains>` as the last progress line. Tick a goal only when its done-when is truly met. Progress lines follow `<id>: <what + decisions + doubts> — <short sha> — <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM>`. Commit on `lane/forge-wish` only: `feat(forge-wish): FW10 …`. Never push, never touch main.
- **Order.** FW10 first. It must be reviewed and checkpointed by Monday morning, so reach a clean, building checkpoint of it before you start anything else. Then FW11, FW12, FW13.
- **Your own servers and ports.** Run your Forge service on a free port in 5170 to 5189 (check with `ss -ltn` first, and avoid 5187), and your own realm against your own database schema (`REALM_DATABASE_SCHEMA=realm_forge_wish`, never the live schema) with its own founded directory and Forge data directory under `/tmp/chatdom-forge-wish/`. A stream-online fact for your realm comes from its authenticated test ingress, the way `packages/testing/live-evening` does it. Write every port you use in progress. Start servers only for a test or a lease, and stop them afterwards.
- **Codex usage per test wish.** Every real wish spends the same limit the development sessions use. Keep it bounded:
  - Develop against controlled providers and stored contact sheets; tune the gates by rerunning only the result gate on stored sheets.
  - At most 30 real wishes per session, at most 2 in flight at once during tests.
  - Run FW10's 20-wish set at most once per checkpoint and at most 3 times in all; FW11's 50-wish set once at the end.
  - Log per wish: model calls, seconds per call, and tokens when the CLI reports them, in your private data directory; put only totals and medians in evidence.
  - If you see a rate-limit or usage message, stop sending wishes at once, write `blocked: codex limit` with the time, and carry on with work that doesn't need the model.
- **Look at it on a real GPU.** Use `chatdom-pc-lease` (read its header): one lane at a time, at most 10 minutes. Inside the leased command, serve your build on 127.0.0.1:5192 and your realm on 5191, then call `chatdom-pc shot http://localhost:5192/<path> <out.png>` with the full URL. `pc shot` can't type, so give the kingdom a dev parameter that replays a stored real wish result (an approved draft from your own runs) at a chosen ritual phase. If the tunnel is down (exit 3), take one short SwiftShader still under the shared capture lock (`packages/testing/capture-lock.mjs`, held under 5 minutes) and say so; SwiftShader frame times are never performance evidence. Open every still and judge it against `chatdom/Quality.md` and `Artstyle-Soul-Feel.md`.
- **Server load.** The host has 12 cores shared with other live services. Run builds, tests and the contact-sheet raster under `nice -n 10`. Typecheck and test only the packages and files you touched (`pnpm exec vitest run <paths>`, the touched package's typecheck); never the full suite, never `pnpm verify`. No long recordings on the CPU.
- **No SIMULATED labels.** Remove the chips in your scope (the anvil card, the benches, the ritual view and their READMEs). Public surfaces show real data or nothing. Dev benches may show staged fixtures, unlabelled, and are never reachable as if they were real. The `source: 'simulated'` field in Forge provenance is a data contract, not a label: leave it.
- **Seams.** Read `SEAMS.md` before touching the HUD slot, the wardrobe or a scene mount. Never edit another lane's files. If a seam must change, write it in both lanes' `SEAMS.md` and add a `needs:` line. For main-owned code (shared exports, the realm reducer) write `needs:` with the patch spelled out and keep going on what doesn't depend on it.
- **Privacy.** A wish's words never enter a public event, the ledger, logs, evidence or the repo. Refusal reasons go only to the maker.
- **Evidence.** Stills and sheets go in `work/evidence/forge-wish/` (PNGs under about 2 MB, clips under about 10 MB). End each session with what you verified, how, where the stills are and what you doubt.
- **Repo text.** No thread ids, and no names of development tools in commits or files.
- **Three strikes.** If the same failure happens three times, stop, write `blocked: <what you learned>` and move to another part of the goal.
