# 7 Things to do: Always something to do, together

**End state:** There is always something to do with the people you care about: fish from the pier and fill the Almanac, sit at a tavern table with chat and the streamer, gather at the campfire at night to find the wolf. Everything you catch, win or keep goes into your bag, onto your shelf or into your home.

**Today:** Today walking earns nothing. There is no inventory, the tavern is an exterior with no inside, the campfire is parked half-done and there is no pier.
