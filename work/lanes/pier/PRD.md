## The part

Walk down to the water at dusk and there is a pier. Its planks run out over the lake on stout piles, lanterns on posts down both rails, a rod rack at the landing, a lectern holding a thick book under a little roof, and a chalk board where today's best catch is written up. The lanterns warm as the light goes. Someone is already at the end with a line out, and a few small floats in the colours of chatters' lights bob in a loose fan off the head.

You take a willow rod from the rack. It goes into your hand and onto the first slot of your hotbar, and its card says where it came from: "Taken from the rack at Willowmere pier on 2 November." You look out over the water, hold to draw the cast back, and let go. The line sings out, the float lands with a soft plop and rings spread. Then nothing, and the water laps, and the lanterns hum, and you wait. The float twitches, once, twice, and dips hard with a splash: strike now. You set the hook and the rod bends double. The fish runs and you give it line, it tires and you reel, the line creaks when you push too hard. Then it breaks the surface, leaps, and swings up onto the planks: a Copper Perch, 412 g. A paper tag slides in with its name, its weight and one line of lore, and the Almanac seal stamps: "New in your Almanac." It goes into your bag, a stack of perch with a story: who caught it, where and when.

The fish depend on the place. A mountain tarn, a marsh lake and a harbour on the sea hold different species, and so does the hour: the Lantern Eel only comes up after dark, the Mistfin Trout only bites in the morning mist. Each kingdom's water has its own share of the 24 to 30 species, from the common perch to four legends that people talk about, so the Almanac of one kingdom never looks like another's, and some fish are only ever caught somewhere else.

Chat fishes too. Type `!fish` and a little float in your light's colour drops in off the end of the pier, on stream, for everyone to see. A while later it dips, a fish leaps, and a tag rises over the pier: "saoirse_k caught a Bell Minnow." A first for the kingdom gets the director's eye and a wax seal. The legends are too strong for a chat line: they take the float and are gone, and the tag says only a walker could have held it.

Every catch fills the kingdom's Almanac: a bound book with a beautiful page for each species, the fish drawn from its real voxel model, its name and lore, where and when it bites, and who caught it first in this kingdom and when. Unknown fish are silhouettes with a riddle. Rare catches become numbered keepsakes: a mounted fish on a carved plaque, "No. 3 of the Moonlit Pike in Willowmere, caught by Juniper at the pier at dusk on 2 November". They go on your keepsake shelf at home, where nothing with a story is ever thrown away.

Underneath all of it is the item foundation the kingdom has never had: every thing a person owns has a record that says what it is, where it came from (who, where and when) and where it lives now (worn, in hand, on the lot, on the shelf, in the bag, or put away). The bag holds stacks, the hotbar holds tools, the shelf holds keepsakes, and the history of every thing is kept. Fishing is the first thing to write into it. The tavern games, the campfire, the Forge and the wardrobe write into it next, through a small typed API that this lane owns and keeps stable.

You own the how and make the design calls. Be ambitious, make it feel wonderful in the hands, and keep everything that works today working while you build.

**Today, honestly.**
- There is no inventory code anywhere. `chatdom/Tech.md` §12 plans `item_copy` and ledger tables; they were never built. No realm table, contract or UI holds a person's things. Forged pieces live only as attachments in the kingdom's private state.
- There is no fishing and no Almanac. Nothing in the realm, the web or the content mentions catches or species.
- There is no pier you can walk on. The places-water lane built a modular voxel pier (`createPier` in `packages/content/src/places/water/pier/`: bays, a head with a bell post and lantern, 'hearth' and 'tide' styles), boats and a boathouse, exported and registered (`BOATHOUSE_BLUEPRINTS`, `BOAT_BLUEPRINTS`). A 'tide' pier is drawn only for unclaimed Genesis sketches with a coastal harbour (`apps/web/src/genesis/water-content.ts`), as scenery with no collider. The kingdom-land geography has a `water:pier` site on its lake (`packages/world/src/kingdom-land/geography.ts`), and the grand land's harbour kit has a `pier` quay section; neither stands in any founded kingdom.
- Walking happens only inside the founded scene's collision: `compileFoundedScene` over the founded plan, 32 x 32 chunks of 16 m, with unknown space solid. Anything a walker stands on must be a placement in the founded scene, assembled by `assembleFoundedScene` in `apps/realm/src/founded/scene.ts` (civic growth adds its bridge over the lake that way; `arrivalFurnishings` is the pattern for a fixed piece). The founded plan's landform has a lake (`plan.landform.lake`).
- The grand land is on only for tobi4s_ (kingdom `129634568`), on grand-world's branch, not on main. Everything else, and main itself, is the old land.
- Chat reaches the realm: Herald forwards each ordinary chat message (everything that is not `!forge`) with its text parts to `POST /internal/realm/herald`. `RealmEveningBridge.accept` waits until the chatter has a lit window (`speechHost`) and relays it as a ten-second speech bubble. There is no chat command handler in the realm; a `!fish` today would show only as a bubble.
- Walkers talk to their kingdom room through `intent` messages, but `RealmIntentSchema` is a closed union in `packages/shared` that this lane may not change. There is no channel a feature can add its own room messages on. The stream view does not join the room: it reads the public watch feed (`/api/realm/watch/:kingdomId`, server-sent events).
- There is no shared kingdom clock for the hour (decision 15; the cycle length is still open, and the page's hour is a URL parameter) and no weather anywhere. The realm serves its process time at `/api/realm/clock`.
- Home interiors are only a type sketch (home-interiors lane, parked); entering a home works as a zone, but there is no furnished room to put a shelf in.
- play's HUD slots (`apps/web/src/play/slots.ts`: ledger pages, HUD pieces, arrival steps) are being built tonight; they have no hotbar region, no held-tool hook and no key bindings for other lanes yet.

## Decisions

Tobias's answers (`context/decisions.md`) override the vision files, the specs, AGENTS.md and the ralph-lane skill wherever they disagree.
- **10, launch activities.** Fishing and the Almanac at a pier at launch. Foraging and gardening are not at launch.
- **9, the core loop.** Presence, plus gather, make and place. Fishing is the first "gather"; the item foundation is what "make" and "place" will stand on.
- **11, walking counts as presence while live.** A walker standing at the pier for twenty minutes stays present and their window stays lit; check it.
- **12, offline play is everything but live beats.** Walkers fish, fill the Almanac and arrange the shelf while the stream is offline. `!fish` comes from chat, so it happens only while chat is flowing.
- **13, chat-only people are lights only.** A chat fisher has no body: their float is their light's colour and their tag rises over the pier.
- **15, a shared kingdom clock.** Fishing reads its hour band from the one kingdom clock. It does not exist yet, so the realm maps its own time (`/api/realm/clock`) through one documented function in `packages/shared/src/fishing.ts` and writes `needs: grand-world kingdom clock export`; when the clock lands, switching is one line.
- **No invisible weather.** There is no weather in the kingdom today. The water mood (clear, breeze, mist, rain) is deterministic per kingdom and time, shown at the pier, and offered to grand-world for the sky with a `needs:` line. No fish ever needs a mood nobody can see.
- **25, no SIMULATED labels.** Public surfaces show real data or nothing. Staged fish, catches and Almanacs live only on the `/-/pier` dev bench, unlabelled, and are never reachable as a real kingdom.
- **Money (ChatDom §8, principle 4).** Nothing for sale buys an advantage. No paid rods, bait, luck or extra lines; Bits, subs, channel points, rank and trust never change a cast, a bite or the odds. A tool gets better by doing, never by paying. Legends have the same odds for everyone.
- **Things (World-Tree §4, rules 9 and 10).** A thing lives in one place: worn, in hand, on the lot, on the shelf, in the bag, or put away. Nothing with a story is destroyed; only spare stacks of gathered things can be let go, and letting a fish go puts it back in the water. Only moments are scarce, so keepsakes are numbered and nothing else is. Keepsakes, placed, held and gathered things belong to the kingdom they came from; worn things travel with the person. At home things are only given; swapping is between kingdoms and not part of this lane.
- **Stacks and stories.** Ordinary catches stack by species (the stack keeps its count, its best weight and where and when it was first and last caught). Anything a person could tell apart, a rare catch, a first, a keepsake, a tool, is its own record with its own story.
- **Server authority.** The realm decides every bite and every catch, and replays the fight before it believes one. The browser draws and predicts, never grants.
- **Names.** Public pages show only approved public names (the realm's approved names). A moderated person's name is hidden from public pages; their own record stays theirs. Fish names and lore are authored content, never free text.
- **The pier is a founded placement**, so the deck is in the collision world on both ends and under the existing revision handshake. Its site is a pure function of the founded plan (or grand-world's anchor), so it never moves.
- **1, a public launch.** Monday's merged build comes first: PI10 is reviewed and checkpointed by Monday morning.

## How to work

- **Start of every session.** Run `git merge --no-edit main` and resolve conflicts inside your scope only; for anything outside it, stop and write a progress note. - **Goals in order.** Work on the first unticked goal. PI10 must be reviewed and checkpointed by Monday morning; the integrator merges reviewed checkpoints continuously from tonight. Pull work forward from a later goal only when it makes the current one true. Never pick from Backlog.
- **Checkpoints.** A goal can take many sessions. End every session with everything building and working, a commit on `lane/pier` (`feat(pier): PI10 <outcome>`, decisions in the body), and one progress line: `<id>: <what changed, decisions, doubts> — <short sha> — <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM>`, followed by `next: <id> <what is done> / <what remains>`. Tick a goal only when its whole done-when bar is met and you have looked at the evidence. Merge main again before each checkpoint. Never push and never touch `main`. When all goals are ticked, write `LANE COMPLETE`.
- **Your own stack.** Test end to end against the real code.
