# launch — chatdom.io works for real people

Spec: none. This PRD, `ops/goals/ROUND.md` (Pace and Wrap-up) and `work/lanes/launch/SEAMS.md` (create it) are the contract · Background: every round lane's PRD and SEAMS in `work/lanes/{front-door,atlas-map,grand-world,play,forge-wish,pier,gather,raid-show,panel-window}/`, `chatdom/Quality.md` (a world that works, the slop list), `chatdom/Safety.md` · Branch: `lane/launch`, cut from `lane/integrator` · Worktree: `../worktrees/launch` · Engine: commits its own checkpoints on `lane/launch`; the integrator merges it like any lane.

What is live: chatdom.io is the public build of `lane/integrator` (gateway `scripts/public/start.mjs` on 5480, the realm on 5197 with the grand land, herald 5194, the Forge 5195, Atlas data 5193). It is redeployed by the operator from `lane/integrator` after merges settle. There is no other production; this is what people will use.

Boundaries: you are the one lane allowed to cross seams, because your job is that the seams hold. Fix small breaks where they are (a wrong route, a missing allowlist entry, a header, a broken import, a missing empty state, a dev string on screen), in as few lines as possible, and add a line under `## from launch` in the owning lane's `SEAMS.md` so its worker knows. Anything bigger than a small fix goes to the owner as a `needs:` line in its SEAMS.md and in your progress, and you move on. Never redesign another lane's work. Start every session with `git merge --no-edit lane/integrator` (keep both sides in conflicts, rerun typecheck). Never touch the live services, containers, routes or the live realm's data: deploying is the operator's job. When something needs a deploy change, write exact steps under `deploy:` in your SEAMS.md and the operator runs them. Reading chatdom.io over HTTPS and using it in a browser as a visitor is fine and expected. Never post in any Twitch chat but tobi4s_, and only through the existing mock or recording transports in tests.

## Goals

- [ ] **LA10 — A stranger can use chatdom.io end to end.**
  Outcome: walk chatdom.io as a first-time visitor on Tobias's RTX 5080 (through pc-lease) and at phone width, and make every step work: the front door and its story; the Atlas, searching a channel and opening its card; "Enter the land" into tobi4s_'s kingdom on the grand land (buildings, homes, water, light); Twitch sign-in and coming back to the same place; walking, the HUD, minimap and lantern path; a wish at the Forge that becomes a piece; fishing at the pier and the Almanac; the tavern portal, a game, the campfire at night; the passport with raid stamps; the panel view at `/panel`; the stream view. For each break, fix it (small) or hand it over (big). Remove every developer trace a visitor could see: placeholder text, SIMULATED labels, test channels, stack traces, raw JSON errors, console errors on load. Every failure a visitor can hit gets a calm, worded state. Keep `noindex` until Tobias says launch.
  Quality bar: a person who has never heard of Chatdom gets from the front door to walking in a kingdom and doing one real thing without help, in under a minute, on a laptop and on a phone. Nothing on screen looks unfinished.
  Done when: a checklist in `work/lanes/launch/WALK.md` with every step above marked working, fixed (commit) or handed over (owner and needs line); stills of each step from the 5080 at 1440×900 and from a 390×844 viewport; zero console errors on the main path; the touched files' tests pass.

- [ ] **LA11 — Ready for people, and easy to run.**
  Outcome: chatdom.io holds up when people arrive. A niced load run against a local copy of the combined build (never against chatdom.io): 50 visitors walking in one kingdom and 200 on the Atlas, with the realm's tick, memory and the gateway measured, and anything that falls over fixed or handed over. Public routes have sane rate limits and caching (immutable assets, no-store on personal data); a restart of any service brings it back without losing anyone's facts; logs say what happened in one line per event. Write `docs/launch/RUNBOOK.md`: what runs where, how to deploy and roll back, health checks, what to do when each service is down, and a short list of what only Tobias can do before opening the doors (the extension id and secret, raid scopes, the Atlas founding key, legal review, lifting noindex).
  Quality bar: someone who did not build it can run it from the runbook alone.
  Done when: the load numbers and fixes are in progress; the runbook is committed; one restart drill of the realm and the gateway on a local copy, recorded.
