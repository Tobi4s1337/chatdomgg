## The part

The grand land is the end version of every kingdom. Open tobi4s_'s stream and you are looking at his island: a coast rising out of a reflecting sea, mountain ranges with banded rock strata and snow on the high slopes, waterfalls dropping into a mere, forests you can tell apart by their silhouettes (autumn gold, pine, cherry, a fantasy teal), patchwork fields, the castle on its rock with its banners, the harbour with its ships and lighthouse, and in the middle of it the real streets of Willowmere, where every citizen's home stands exactly where it always stood. As the hours turn the light goes from cool dawn to warm noon to the long gold of evening, and at dusk the windows of the people who are here light up one by one. The stream, the walk, the homes and doors, the Forge square, the moments, the raid road, the Chronicle and the front page all stand on this one land. There is one land per kingdom and one source of truth for it, and the old small land is retired.

The land is made once. When a kingdom is founded, the server raises its world from the channel's identity and Genesis profile under a versioned recipe with a hash, and bakes the meshes for every region at every level of detail. It stores them compressed and serves them like the founded scene: immutable, content-addressed, cacheable forever. A viewer never generates land. Their own street arrives first and is on screen in under 3 seconds; the rest streams in behind them nearest first while they already walk; the browser keeps what it has. The same kingdom is the same land on every screen, today and in a year, and a new recipe version never changes a founded kingdom unless someone deliberately migrates it.

Opening a kingdom is a moment, not a wait. A parchment map unrolls, your street is inked onto it, the ink rises into voxels and your window lights. Every beat is bound to a real step (the receipt checked, your street's regions downloaded, the ground ready, the far land streaming), so the progress you see is the truth. The type is beautiful. The same loader opens the kingdom, the stream view and the grand view, and other surfaces can mount it.

It is fast without giving up any beauty. The stream holds 60 fps at 1080p on an RTX-class GPU and beside a game in OBS; walking feels the same; a mid laptop with integrated graphics runs it at 30 fps or better. The speed comes from the engine: baked far land as low-cost distance meshes or impostors, instanced trees and props, culling, packed vertices, few draw calls, reflections and shadows sized to what is on screen. On weaker devices the view distance and the effects scale first. The palette, the light, the voxel detail near you and the dusk windows never do.

Every channel gets a world of its own. Genesis picks its landform (island, mountain valley, river delta, lake country, cliff coast, highland), its biome and forest species from the channel's categories, its crest colours on banners and roofs, its emotes in stone and glass, and its landmarks, so no two kingdoms look alike and a still of any of them stands next to the Eldermere reference without embarrassment. And the land grows with its people. The whole world exists from founding, so nothing ever shifts, but a hamlet sees only its own valley clear, with the rest under sea mist and cloud. As people arrive, milestones open more of it: the mist lifts off the next valley, the mountain pass is cut, the harbour is built, a bridge reaches the outer island, the castle gains towers. Each opening is a beat on stream and a page in the Chronicle, so the community sees that they made the world bigger.

You own the how and make the design calls. Be ambitious, think it through end to end, and keep everything that works today working while you build.

**Today, honestly.**
- The standalone grand land exists and is stunning at `/-/grand?channel=tobi4s_` (about 79 M cells and 2.3 k trees for tobi4s_, 128 m regions at four levels of detail, worker meshing, planar sea reflection, clouds, golden hour and indigo dusk; stills in `work/evidence/grand-kingdom/`). It shows SIMULATED chips and uses Genesis defaults where no channel data was observed. It has never been measured on a real GPU. On the software renderer it drew 9.2 M triangles in 222 draws per frame across main, reflection and shadow passes.
- This branch (52bb8cc) hosts that land inside the kingdom scene through `apps/web/src/kingdom/grand-land/` with one terrain owner, no second render loop, the kingdom's own light and post stack, exact half-metre floor grading under the installed region (checked column by column), the physical regions pinned to full detail, and one raster water surface. It switches on for every founded kingdom (`useGrandLand = !!options.founded` in `scene.ts`), not only for tobi4s_.
- The land is generated in the viewer's browser: about 25 s of CPU for 2560 x 2560 columns for kingdom 129634568, then the 16 physical regions mesh in about 5 s. On the software renderer the real stream reached readiness in about 56 s (it used to fail at 300 s) with 38 regions shown, 292 draws and 3.2 M triangles in the main pass plus 275 draws and 3.2 M triangles in one reflection, and a 164 MB high post stack. A screenshot of the loaded frame still timed out even with the render loop frozen. No loaded frame has ever been viewed. No walk, door, Forge, moment or raid arrival has been checked on it. It has never run on a real GPU.
- The seat still uses the old built-home centroid and footprint, so growth can move or resize the land around the town. Recipe inputs are mutable: the tier and the first home's style are read from the live plan, and the identity hash covers the whole plan JSON, so it changes whenever a home is added. There is no persistent recipe.
- Main is 46 commits ahead of this branch. They include the stable reservation-based growth seating (grand-kingdom-w2 3e5061f, merged as 00e888e: growth-stable envelope and capped land size, castle crags, paved streets, reserved lawns, lowered water bands, deep wall footings), the grand artisans' kits (9a17930), civic life, the native Chronicle and `work/HANDOFF-LANES.md`.

Outside that region everything is a solid barrier. The grand walls and castle rock in `packages/world/src/grand/kingdom/` are presentation only and have no colliders.

## Decisions

Tobias's answers (`context/decisions.md`) override spec 56, AGENTS.md and the ralph-lane skill wherever they disagree.
- **47, the grand land is the end version.** Worlds are generated on this level for every kingdom, grow with the community and are optimised for speed without the quality going down. This changes spec 56 from a standalone producer to the kingdom's only land and retires the old small land. Tobias, tonight: "have the worlds generated around this, on this level (ambitious!), grow with the community somehow, and performance optimised (without making the quality go down). It is stunning, so it should be our end version."
- **E10, quality is not traded for speed.** View distance and effects scale first; palette, light, near voxel detail and the dusk windows never do.
- **29, growth.** Both: the epic island land and automatic growth.
- **25, no SIMULATED labels anywhere.** Public surfaces show real data or nothing. Staged fixtures live only on dev benches, unlabelled, and are never reachable as if they were real. Remove the chips in this lane's scope (`apps/web/src/grand/**`, `grand/kingdom/**`, `kingdom/scene.ts`, `build-scene.ts`, `presence-feed.ts`, `forge-square-content.ts`, the ground-dressing, terrain-art and trees benches, `stream/dock-fixture.ts`, `kingdom-land/**`). This overrides the ralph-lane rule and the AGENTS.md line about labelling simulations.
- **15, a real day cycle on a shared kingdom clock.** Every hour must be beautiful, which is why GW10's stills cover four hours. The cycle length is still open (`context/open-questions.md`).
- **13, chat-only people are lights only** (their window and a bubble over their home); only walkers have bodies. The hamlet feels alive through real lights, keepers and places, never invented residents.
- **11 and 12.** Walking counts as presence while live, and everything except live beats works offline, so the land and its loader work offline too.
- **44.** Raiders who are playing walk in as characters: raid arrivals must land on supported grand floor.
- **28 and 36.** The Way Home intro, the minimap and the lantern path home are play's; they read this lane's ground query and land map.
- **3 and 19.** An unclaimed kingdom is a private sketch; Atlas placement is computed from the channel id and stored only on claim. Key the land recipe by the full channel id, and persist it at founding or claim.
- **31, stay on this host.** Baking and serving run here, under the server-load rules below.
- **40.** Seasons are later; when they come they dress the land and never reroll it.
- **1.** This is a public launch; Monday's merged build comes first, so GW10 is reviewed and checkpointed by Monday morning.
- **Spec 56 rules that still hold:** nobody ever moves (lots, doors, placements and plot ids keep their coordinates and hashes); the installed settlement is exact and the land fits around it; unknown space is solid; one identity chain from recipe to scene receipt; WebGL2 is the baseline (GPU culling only as a measured option); screen-error level of detail with balanced, gap-free transitions; the budgets table; zero per-frame network traffic for land and sky.

- **Lanes this round:** grand-world, play, forge-wish, front-door, atlas-map, raid-show, panel-window, teaser. They all run at the same time. Each one owns only its own scope.
