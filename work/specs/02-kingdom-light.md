# Spec 02: A kingdom lit by its people

Status: first consumer contracts frozen 2026-10-30 after independent Astra high review; scene implementation in progress.

Vision: `chatdom/ChatDom.md` §§2–6; `chatdom/Quality.md` rules 1–2, 5, 8–11, 16; `chatdom/Artstyle-Soul-Feel.md` §§6, 13–16; `chatdom/World-Tree.md` §§2, 5.

## What and why

A dark town at dusk. One citizen's own window warms; nearby, their name appears. Arrivals ripple across the streets,
hundreds of windows fill with people, and the castle beacon rises into the clouds. The town has real terrain,
roads, full reserved lots, tenure quarters, homes and a castle, generated by one system for hamlet and town.
The first feed is visibly SIMULATED. It exercises the same private observations, leases, projection and public
adapter as Herald. No renderer fabricates presence from a population number.

## Current state

`packages/shared/src/voxel.ts` and `presence.ts` contain this small freeze, with representative and hostile
fixtures in `world-fixtures.ts` and `world-contracts.test.ts`. `packages/world/src/presence.ts` implements the
pure private reducer, minute sampler and public projection. `packages/voxel/src/` implements palette packing,
AO/sky meshing, flood lighting and owned worker jobs. Atlas remains independent and is with the team.

## Rules

1. **R1 — Units:** metres at scene boundaries; +Y up. Terrain 0.5 m, builds/props 0.125 m, avatar lattice 0.0625 m.
   Integer cells, floor division for negative coordinates, 32³ chunks, x contiguous. No body implementation here.
2. **R2 — Material identity:** u16 stable material keys; zero is non-occluding air. Palette offsets are not IDs.
   Registry revision is a mesh dependency. Kingdom style IDs resolve through `packages/content/src/materials.ts`.
3. **R3 — Data before geometry:** packed chunks use 0/1/2/4/8-bit palette indexes. CPU occupancy is authoritative.
   Meshes carry own, material and all halo revisions. Unknown neighbours differ from known empty neighbours.
4. **R4 — AO and sky:** the one-cell halo includes edges and corners. AO uses the two-side/diagonal rule;
   triangle orientation and merges preserve interpolation. Static sky is computed beyond each chunk's roof.
5. **R5 — Worker isolation:** jobs own submit-time snapshots. Bound queues, discard stale results, cancel obsolete
   work and settle promises on failure. Authoritative occupancy is never detached to transfer a visual job.
6. **R6 — Light field:** presence alters a texture and a real six-neighbour flood volume. Walls block it, doors
   admit it, removals darken it and overlapping sources survive. The field is R8; a common warm core is shaded
   identically for everyone. Reserve under 5 MiB for the local solve and texture copies at the supported maximum.
7. **R7 — Presence:** union chatters/chat/panel/walker leases per identity. Ninety-second evidence leases and a
   180-second confirmed stream lease. Chatters-only people remain unnamed. Recovered historical events cannot
   revive light or earn minutes. Fresh reconciliation uses its actual new observation time and live origin.
8. **R8 — Time and moderation:** stale/out-of-order/duplicate/future evidence cannot refresh a lease. Complete
   rosters remove only evidence they supersede; partial results never claim disappearance. Restrictive holds
   merge by strongest duration, bans need a newer restore, and moderation restore never reverses forget consent.
9. **R9 — No fabricated progress:** minute samples are private, deduplicated eligibility, not durable rewards.
   Persistence/economy are not frozen here. No catch-up credit for historical minutes.
10. **R10 — Public adapter:** `PresenceFeed` supplies complete `KingdomPresenceFrame` projections. Slots are visual
    indexes, not identities or deeds; no private person IDs, chat bodies, moderation or session IDs enter the feed.
    Unavailable transport visibly loses freshness and expires lights. Simulation is labelled on every surface.
11. **R11 — Settlement:** full 8 m frontage × 16 m depth × 20 m height reserved. Follow-year corridors grow at
    their ends without moving old addresses. Tier growth moves the back fence; late followers use their own year.
    No subscription-based brightness or quarter assignment. The demonstration's tenure is explicitly simulated.
12. **R12 — Composition:** noon, dusk and night are coherent sky/material/fog grades. Warm light never blows out.
    Crafted clay, restrained bevel/grain, a clear castle silhouette, a horizon and purposeful foreground detail.
13. **R13 — Sound:** input cues, first light, arrival wave and beacon have distinct procedural sound, unlocked by
    a gesture, with variations and a bounded mix. No externally sourced uncredited sound.
14. **R14 — Evidence:** fixed cameras at 1440×900 and 390×844 for all three hours; inspect every shot. Capture the
    light wave as internal build evidence. Serve preview on 5190 for real GPU review; software fps proves nothing.

## Contracts

Frozen: `VoxelLattice`, `VoxelMaterial`/registry, `VoxelAsset`, `PackedVoxelChunk`, `MeshStamp`, `VoxelBlueprint`
(half-open fill/erase boxes and window anchors on the exterior facade plane), home tiers/reserved lot dimensions,
private `PresenceInput`, public `KingdomPresenceFrame`, and `PresenceFeed`. `packages/world` owns reducer semantics.

Maximum blueprint axis 256 cells; padded volume at most 16,777,216 cells. This is an allocation guard, not a target
for every prop. Full `CVOX` publication, provenance, interactive pieces, draft editing, rig and scene travel remain
unfrozen until their consumers start. This turn's packed chunk is not falsely advertised as that complete format.

## Scope

Main: shared/world/voxel/render core, integration, core content fixtures, tests, review and commits. Sub-agents
inside main own disjoint algorithm, atmosphere, model and verification files. Herald and authored content batches
become separate lanes on this freeze. Atlas's assigned folders remain owned by its existing lane.

## Acceptance

`pnpm verify` must pass. Relevant unit fixtures prove negative chunks, palette corruption, AO orientation, halo
seams, roof-crossing sky, occluded multi-source light, removal, stale workers, lease union, anonymity, moderation,
minute deduplication, and immutable non-overlapping lots. The runtime must show worker-built geometry, real field
uploads, no mesh rebuild for presence, and a meaningful ready frame, not a completed timer.

Scene screenshots and short internal capture are recorded in the final review with exact commands and paths.
Cold loading, source loss and context loss must be designed; remaining device-performance evidence is explicit.

## Quality bar

Beat the studied `f-lanternmere` night/dusk frames: clearer neighbourhoods and castle, gentler terrain, street-level
warmth and source-sized glow. A lone window must feel personal before the whole town becomes a spectacle.

## Not now

Walking avatars and build mode are the next brief. No guessed drafts, rigs, rewards, full Herald actions or
Genesis spell contracts are required for this consumer. The kingdom generator is not claimed to be live Genesis.

## Decisions

- **2026-10-30:** first consumer freeze only. Independent Astra high review found and verified fixes to permanent
  holds, anonymity ties, queued-buffer mutation and asset allocation limits. R8 clarifies recovered vs fresh
  reconciliation. Public projections carry visual slots, never private identity. Bounded R8 light plus shared
  warm RGB fits the volume budget without hundreds of real-time lights.
- **2026-10-30:** WebGL2 GLSL on Three's WebGLRenderer is the proven renderer path. Reference TSL/WebGPU atmosphere
  is a technique source, not a drop-in dependency. Eight-byte vertices retain material/AO/sky; batching uses
  previously reserved bits for asset-local chunk offsets without changing authoritative chunk data.
