# Soul checkpoint visual review

Fresh captures from the merged checkpoint are inspected with `view_image`, not inferred from source or readiness metadata. These are SIMULATED review frames. They do not demonstrate the main-owned live kingdom/walk/stream binding.

- **Dusk, 1440×900:** opened. The hard ground/sky polygon edge is gone. Ordered door/window openings survive meshing; roofs and framing read as authored voxel homes. Civic lamps pool on the road and walls; citizen windows remain unlit. The distant environment remains sparse and its simple peaks are weaker than the homes. This is an isolated review district, not final main terrain.
- **Phone, 390×844:** opened. Controls, labels, time and sound affordance fit with no document overflow. The active lamplighter is present beside a visited lamp, labelled by occupation. The street remains visible above the control panel, though the wide composition crops peripheral homes; this does not verify the live walking camera.
- **Stream composition, 1920×1080:** opened. Review controls are hidden; SIMULATED remains visible. The world is readable and the civic pools carry the dusk mood. The title belongs to this review; this is not a claim that the actual stream director has mounted soul.

Each still has its own `capture-<name>.json` with build fingerprint, lease duration, sampled frame, draw/geometry/texture counters and overflow check. Dusk measured 53 calls, 108282 triangles and 630 points; those are this fixture's counters, not real-GPU frame timing or a live-scene budget pass. The successful worker boot is demonstrated by the meaningful frame, not merely by the CPU worker test.

**Night, 1440×900:** opened. The civic pools and unlit absent-citizen windows read. Stars are not visible in this overhead framing, so this still does not prove the clear-star-field requirement; that remains a specific main sky/walk verification need. The night ground is muted green, weaker than the intended cool-blue signature mood. No visual completion is claimed for the whole end state.

**Dawn, 1440×900:** opened. The baker, occupation plaque, bread cart and bakery chimney smoke read; citizen windows stay dark. The fixed natural-weather seed produces rain (0.8), mist (0.48) and no snow at 18.5°C. In this still the point-based rain reads as pale flecks and the mist is subtle; neither should be claimed as final weather art quality from this view alone. Smoke reads as a stylised column.

**Noon, 1440×900:** opened. The smith and harbour master are visible and occupation-labelled; baker and lamplighter are indoors. Daylight shows the authored openings and roof forms. The isolated review does not supply a real forge yard or quay for these workers, and its broad contact-shadow patches are approximate. Their real civic placements and main lighting remain integration requirements, not demonstrated here.

**Rain, 1440×900:** opened. Roof wetting and small street puddles are present; the sky is subdued relative to noon. Rain still reads as fine pale flecks rather than convincing streaks in a still, and the contact-shadow patches are approximate. This is an explicit art-quality limitation, not a final rain-quality pass.

**Lamplighter, 1440×900:** opened. At the fixed early-dusk moment one visited lamp pools light and the next remains dark; the occupation-labelled keeper is between them. Later dusk has the complete lit street. The route/visit state is supported by the deterministic tests as well as these views.

**Winter, 1440×900:** opened. Snow sits on exposed upward roof steps and peak caps, and larger flakes distinguish it from the rain sample. The inland scene omits the harbour master. The isolated review's ground remains green; main's proposed packed-voxel terrain snow remains uncompiled/unproven in the actual kingdom.

All nine named stills have now been opened, including desktop, phone and stream compositions. They share one current build fingerprint. All captures took one still per lease and closed their browser/server before release. Their composition and runtime checks do not prove the missing main integration or every art-quality requirement. Audio files and signal measurements do not establish subjective listening quality. The whole SO10 checkbox remains open for the main integration and required complete demonstration.
