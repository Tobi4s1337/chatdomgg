CPU-only Vite SSR profiling of the frozen `/tmp/grand-artisans-candidate`, with the candidate source unchanged. The pre-flora `sitePlacements` call occurs inside `generateGrandLand`; a second `compose` call measures the post-flora path. Times are wall time with a 1 ms V8 sampling profiler and shared-host contention, not stable frame-time measurements.

For Tobi, samples attribute about 2.64 s self time to `shapeLand`, 2.06 s to `erode`, 1.43 s to noise, 0.105 s to `placeBox`, and 0.085 s to `renderClearance`. For Aggman, the equivalent values are 2.77 s, 1.59 s, 1.75 s, 0.463 s, and 0.470 s. These are sampled self times over both placement passes plus generation, not whole-call or additive wall-time estimates. The render-clearance invocation counter was not instrumented and is deliberately null; the sampled function time remains valid. Previous 28–55 s generation observations under concurrent GPU/capture and builds cannot be directly compared to this quieter profile. Terrain/erosion dominate both profiles, so an adapter-only change cannot explain or eliminate all generation time. Mesh time was not profiled here.

Recommended bounded adapter optimization: cache each immutable kit's validated local half-open bounding box for the duration of a single `sitePlacements` call. The only transforms are integer translations and 90-degree cardinal rotations, so rotating the local AABB as one box produces the same extrema as rotating every constituent box. Keep the cache local to the call and compute it through the existing validated `kitBounds` scan on first use; do not cache across calls or mutate kit geometry. Preserve the same guard call order, exact bounds, search order, work accounting, water checks, access checks and collision logic. New ad hoc combined pieces remain evaluated from their own boxes; caching only helps repeated references. This targets `placeBox`/`kitBounds`, not the terrain generator or mesh.

`adoption-perf-bounds.json` validates 760 combinations over 19 real kit variants, negative/positive origins and eight equivalent cardinal turns; it found zero mismatches. For 1,200 local microbenchmark calls, per-box baseline took 239.9 ms and warmed cached local AABBs took 2.0 ms. This is a microbenchmark, not a production speedup claim: first-time cache computation, ephemeral pieces, JIT, guard work and producer costs reduce the real benefit. Actual pre/post-flora selected placements and byte-exact shapes still require before/after source-level comparison, plus all placement regressions, after implementing the cache.
