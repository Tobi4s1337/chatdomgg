# Grand artisan seam after grand/GK11 merge

Read-only inspection of the current lane tree after the main merge and of `../grand-kingdom`. This revises the earlier d175db5 note: the grand producer, standalone renderer, route and GK11 modules are now present here. No hosted page, source outside this evidence file, patch or Git state was changed or tested.

## Critical result

`/-/grand` now exists and consumes the artisan adapter in its **standalone grand land**: `apps/web/src/grand/index.tsx` starts `GrandEngine`; its worker calls `generateGrandLand`; `packages/world/src/grand/kits.ts` eagerly imports `./artisans/index.ts` and installs `GRAND_ARTISAN_KITS.trees` and `.compose`. The worker uses the resulting structures and `landMaterials` for voxelisation; the standalone renderer uses that same `landMaterials` for its palette. This establishes a code path, not proof that any particular kit was placed, looked at or approved in the newly merged route.

The newly merged GK11 **is not wired into that route**. `seatKingdomOnLand` has no non-test caller. `mountGrandKingdom` is called only inside `grand/kingdom/attach.ts`, and `attachGrandKingdom` has no caller. `GrandEngine` and its worker do not carry a seated plan/field, so the generated land is not graded and the GK11 kingdom layer, walls and channel pieces do not mount. The live `apps/web/src/kingdom/scene.ts` remains on its own `loadTerrain(layout)`/`terrainHeight(layout,...)` path; it does not host this grand terrain or these artisan placements. `grand-adoption/pages.ts` is a bounded sparse physical-page query, not a terrain producer, host, page transport or attestation; its export is not itself native adoption.

The GK11 content builders contain real native/Genesis asset assembly logic, but the selected channel path does **not** read an installed live scene: `loadGrandKingdomContent` builds tobi4s_ as a labelled SIMULATED founding, and the other three as potential Genesis compositions. `foundedKingdomContent(scene, meta)` can map an explicitly supplied live founded scene with identities intact, but this helper is not wired to the standalone route or installed realm. No claim about actual hosted channel assemblies follows from their existence.

## Exact producer differences and existing patches

* `generateGrandLand` still runs `placeTrees` first and calls `composeStructures(bare)` afterward. The default `GRAND_ARTISAN_KITS` composer checks existing tree envelopes, so this ordering can reject otherwise suitable supported structures. `adoption-sites.needs.patch` remains relevant **for an independent GK10 rendering**: it computes placements before flora and supplies their rotated XZ bounds to the tree producer as protected areas. Do not use this default render-only composer as authority in an installed realm. GK11 must first establish the actual seat and installed-scene guard, reserve full-piece sites, and fit on the correct supported field; blindly reserving the provisional town/harbour locations before seating is not equivalent.
* `voxelize.ts` still uses `strataWarp`, with no `createGeologySampler` or slope input, and its tree cache remains entry-count-only. `kits.ts` still silently collapses palette overflow to its last colour. These are the three changes in `adoption.patch` (geology sampler, 8 MiB tree cache, explicit palette overflow); none has been absorbed in the inspected merged producer. Renderer and worker both obtain their palette/kind tables through `landMaterials`, a seam to retain.
* Both existing patch files passed `patch --batch --dry-run -p1` against a fresh throwaway `/tmp/adoption-merged-check` copy of the **current merged** `kits.ts`, `voxelize.ts`, `generate.ts`, and `flora.ts`. This shows textual applicability only. The patch texts were not applied or overwritten; compilation, GK11 composition, identity preservation, physical support, runtime palette behaviour and visuals remain separate checks.
* The old report's statement that the grand producer and `/-/grand` route are absent is obsolete. No patch is needed for the kit glob itself or the standalone route. The patches do not integrate native ownership, channel content, or seating.

## Safe integration boundary

`seatKingdomOnLand` provides an in-place seating/grading helper and `grandKingdomLand` can expose founded-ground data, but neither is connected to an installed-scene handshake. `clearKingdomStructures` filters **individual structure boxes**, so applying it to partially intersecting authored kits may leave broken pieces; do not treat it as full-piece clearance. Decide and reserve entire fitted kit placements against the actual seated region and all native lots, Forge, roads, growth and revisions before stamping. Bind the exact land recipe/compiler/artifact and coordinate frame to the installed realm identity and verify server/client physical pages before allowing far walking; a visual seat translation or sparse query alone does not establish that authority.

`GRAND_ARTISAN_KITS` exports the tree adapter and mill/quay/lighthouse/ship composer. Artisan citadel, crop-terrace and four-channel `createComposition` geometries are review/study outputs and are not returned by that runtime composer. The producer and scene owners still need to decide how suitable parts are fitted to actual native identities and supported land without replacing homes, castle, Forge or other owned content.

## Whole-piece reservation review

`GrandLand.structures` stores only seven integers per **box**, and `placeBoxes` flattens placements with no piece IDs, placement starts/ends, or source references. Colours also have no placement association. Therefore `clearKingdomStructures` cannot infer whether two boxes belonged to one quay, stair, mill or two independent pieces. A box crossing the reserved region is removed individually; other boxes from that same piece can remain, including elevated deck, sails or detached supports. The packed array is insufficient for safe whole-piece rejection; geometry overlap, material, box adjacency or `piece.id` guesses cannot reconstruct its provenance.

An owner-facing minimal proposal is `work/evidence/grand-artisans/native-piece-reservation.needs.patch`. It adds a pure `retainUnreservedPlacements(placements, reserve)` helper over the **original** `GrandKitPlacement[]` before `placeBoxes`. If any authored box intersects the half-open XZ rectangle, the entire placement is removed; retained objects and ordering are preserved. The helper rejects invalid/unrepresentable input placement coordinates, accepts negative coordinates and exact non-overlapping edge contact, and does not mutate pieces, land or caches. It grants no physical collision or live-scene authority. Its included proposed test exercises a real windmill in all four rotations, plus half-open edge, partial-overlap and invalid-coordinate cases. The patch has not been applied to product source. It passed a dry-run against a fresh copy of this merged tree; an in-memory Vite proof against the exact helper and real current windmill passed all 8 assertions. The included Vitest file and full repository checks have not been run from an applied patch.

Producer owner integration must retain the proposal objects through `seatKingdomOnLand` and call the helper **before** their first `placeBoxes`; pass a conservative rectangle covering the whole changed grading band (`seat.columns` expanded by `seat.band`, clipped to the source field), not merely the inner `KingdomSites.reserve`. Since `gradeSettlement` changes terrain, water and tree positions in that band, also revalidate retained proposal supports against the final graded field and rerun fitting if an altered area can affect them. The exact point at which proposals are created, seated and protected for flora is producer-owned; filtering after a completed composer may reject a first-choice kit without searching an alternative. If only a legacy packed land is available, discard/rebuild its composed structure layer from original proposals or regenerate the land; do not call box-level `clearKingdomStructures` and treat the remainder as whole pieces.

For an independent GK10 render without GK11 seating there is no changed native band to clear, and the existing render-only clearance path remains applicable. In a live installed scene the rectangle helper is only an extra conservative filter: call `sitePlacements` with a real installed-scene guard covering homes, plots, streets, Forge, growth/clearances and the exact revision, and verify supported ground, access and matching server/client physical collision through the installed identity handshake. A render proposal, piece ID, bounding rectangle or this helper does not grant collision or permission to alter a founded kingdom.
