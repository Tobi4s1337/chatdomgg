Its `generate.ts` currently calls `placeTrees` first and `composeStructures(bare)` afterwards. The root composer guards against tree envelopes; this ordering can reject sites that would have been clear before vegetation. The patches and previous isolated-candidate result are not proof of native adoption.

The producer/scene owner must fit suitable parts to actual installed identities and supported land, or retain the existing physical civic geometry; another pass is needed to show how every desired kit becomes visible.

Per spec 56, a real adoption needs the builder's reproducible versioned landscape recipe/artifact and support-preserving frame, a single abortable terrain-only owner inside the existing native scene/camera/renderer/light/post stack, and exact installed `KingdomLandInterface` terrain support without moving home, Forge, castle, citizen or growth identities.
