1. One

    We read public Twitch data

    The map is drawn from what Twitch shows everyone: who is live, in which language, playing what. Nothing private: no passwords, no private messages.

  2. Two

    Languages become continents

    Each big streaming language is a continent; smaller ones share the Isles of Many Tongues. Inside, channels gather into realms by the games they play, and bigger audiences build bigger towns.

  3. Three

    Live streams glow

    When a channel goes live, its place on the map lights up. Inside a founded kingdom, everyone in chat lights a window of their own.

  4. Four

    Every channel is a door

    Open any channel on the map. A founded kingdom lets you walk in, signed in with Twitch, and take a home of your own. The rest is land waiting for its streamer.

What we never collect

  • Your Twitch password. You sign in on Twitch’s own page.
  • Your email address. Twitch tells us only your public name.
  • An archive of your chat. A message shows in the kingdom for a few seconds, then it is gone. Only the words of a !forge wish stay, on the thing they made.
  • Trackers. No analytics, no advertising, no fonts or scripts from anyone else.
