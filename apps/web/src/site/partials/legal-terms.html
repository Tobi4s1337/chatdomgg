How things are checked and removed

Everything made at the Forge passes two checks: the words before anything is made, and the result before anyone else sees it. These checks are run by AI models that judge the thing, not the person. A check can let it pass, keep it private until the channel's moderators have looked at it, or refuse it. When a wish is held or refused, the Forge tells you at once, and the thing never reaches the stream.

Automatic checks only hold or hide a thing. They never touch your home, your place in the kingdom or your Twitch account. People decide the rest:

  • the streamer and the channel's moderators decide what stays in their kingdom, and may remove or hide anything in it;
  • we may remove or hide content anywhere in Chatdom when it breaks these terms or the law, when a kingdom's own people do not act, or when an authority orders us to.

When we restrict something you made and we can reach you, we tell you what we did and why. If you think we got it wrong, write to us and a person will look at it again.
